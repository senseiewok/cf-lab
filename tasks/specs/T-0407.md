## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/lentiviral_basal_integration_persistence.py` modeling lentiviral proviral integration in long-lived airway basal stem cells (KRT5+/TP63+) and long-term promoter epigenetic silencing kinetics:
1. **Basal Progenitor Stem-Cell Retention**: Models proviral genomic integration retention across self-renewing basal stem cells ($t_{1/2} \ge 3,650\ \text{days}$) and clonal transmission to differentiated luminal daughter cells (ciliated/secretory, $t_{1/2} \approx 90\text{--}120\ \text{days}$).
2. **CpG Methylation & Epigenetic Silencing Kinetics**: Simulates DNA methyltransferase (DNMT)-mediated CpG island methylation and histone deacetylation attenuation of transgene expression over successive division cycles across promoter designs (EF1a, MND, synthetic hCFTR).
3. **Proviral Integration Copy-Number Safety**: Evaluates vector copy number (VCN) per cell ($1 \le \text{VCN} \le 4$) balancing therapeutic CFTR channel density against insertional mutagenesis thresholds.
4. **Durable Transepithelial Chloride Secretion**: Couples persistent basal CFTR Band C maturation to steady-state apical chloride current, predicting duration above the therapeutic threshold ($\ge 10\%\ \text{WT}$).
