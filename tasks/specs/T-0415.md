## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Translates the T-0408 biophysical aerosol deposition mechanics model into an interactive browser simulator on `/cf/delivery/` with full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity:
1. **Interactive Morphological Deposition Curve**: Visualizes fractional deposition across Weibel generations 0 to 16 with real-time sliders for MMAD ($1.0\text{--}5.0\ \mu\text{m}$), GSD ($1.5\text{--}2.5$), and inspiratory flow ($15\text{--}60\ \text{L/min}$).
2. **CF Obstruction Toggle**: Simulates progressive luminal narrowing ($0.4\text{--}1.0$), showing dynamic impaction shift into proximal airways and reduced small bronchiole delivery.
3. **Delivery Platform Benchmarking**: Interactive presets for vibrating mesh (VX-522, 4D-710) vs jet nebulizers with dynamic Bronchial Delivery Index (BDI) gauge.
4. **Bilingual Parity**: Complete English and Mexican Spanish (`/es/`) UI copy, ARIA live region support, and strict medical boundary dignity.
