## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/microvillar_glycocalyx_model.py` to quantify the biophysical transport barrier of the apical epithelial microvillar brush and membrane-tethered mucin glycocalyx:
1. **Brush Geometry & Spatial Mesh**: Parameterizes human airway epithelial microvillar architecture (length $L_v \approx 1.0-2.0\ \mu\text{m}$, diameter $d_v \approx 100\ \text{nm}$, center-to-center spacing $s_v \approx 150-250\ \text{nm}$).
2. **Polymer Brush Osmotic Pressure**: Models repulsive steric and entropic osmotic pressures generated by high-molecular-weight transmembrane mucins (MUC1, MUC4, MUC16) resisting particle penetration ($\Pi_{\text{brush}} \approx 10^2 - 10^4\ \text{Pa}$).
3. **Delivery Flux Attenuation**: Calculates size-dependent diffusion attenuation across viral capsids (AAV: ~25 nm), lipid nanoparticles (LNPs: ~80-120 nm), and naked oligonucleotides (ASO: ~2 nm).
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_microvillar_glycocalyx_model.py`.
