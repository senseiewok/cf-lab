## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/u1_snrnp_decoy_kinetics.py` to simulate spliceosome competitive thermodynamics and kinetic exclusion of deep-intronic pseudoexons in CFTR pre-mRNA (specifically focusing on 3849+10kb C->T in intron 22 and 1811+1.6kb A->G in intron 12). The model:
1. Simulates base-pairing thermodynamic stability ($\Delta G^\circ$) of U1 small nuclear RNA (snRNA) 5'-tail with cryptic donor sites versus splice-switching antisense oligonucleotides (SSOs, MOE/PMO chemistries) targeting adjacent Intronic Splicing Silencers (ISS) and Enhancers (ISE).
2. Models mass-action kinetics of U1 snRNP displacement, U2AF65 polypyrimidine tract skipping, and productive exon 22-exon 23 joining.
3. Computes SSO dose-dependent restoration of full-length CFTR mRNA ($EC_{50} \sim 20\text{--}80\text{ nM}$) and transepithelial equivalent chloride conductance.
4. Registers the new module in `DISCOVERY_MODULE_REGISTRY` in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py`.
