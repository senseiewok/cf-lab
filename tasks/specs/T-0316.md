## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/cftr_gquadruplex_stability_model.py` to identify and model thermodynamic stability of putative RNA G-quadruplex (rG4) motifs and stable stem-loops in NM_000492.4:
1. **rG4 Sequence & Thermodynamic Scoring**: Identifies canonical $G_{\ge 2}N_{1-7}G_{\ge 2}N_{1-7}G_{\ge 2}N_{1-7}G_{\ge 2}$ motifs and non-canonical loop variants across CFTR coding sequence and calculates predicted $\Delta G^\circ$ unfolding energies and melting temperatures ($T_m$).
2. **Ribosomal Elongation Kinetics**: Couples RNA secondary structure stability to ribosome dwelling time per codon, identifying putative translation pause sites that promote co-translational domain folding of MSD1, NBD1, and MSD2.
3. **Small-Molecule Perturbation**: Simulates the effect of G-quadruplex stabilizing ligands (e.g. pyridostatin, TMPyP4) vs. DHX36/RHAU RNA helicase activity on full-length CFTR synthesis yield.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_cftr_gquadruplex_stability_model.py` and registration in `cf-research` test suites.
