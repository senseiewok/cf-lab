## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/club_cell_exosome_trafficking.py` to simulate club cell secretory vesicle biogenesis, SCGB1A1 (CCSP/CC16) anti-inflammatory protein secretion, and apical extracellular vesicle (EV) packaging kinetics in bronchiolar epithelium:
1. **Club Cell Vesicular Dynamics**: Models dense-core granule trafficking, docking, and SNARE-mediated fusion at the apical plasma membrane in human bronchiolar club cells.
2. **SCGB1A1 Secretion & Anti-Inflammatory Homeostasis**: Quantifies physiological secretion rates of secretoglobin SCGB1A1 into airway surface liquid (ASL), modeling suppression of cytosolic phospholipase A2 (cPLA2) and oxidative stress in CF airways.
3. **Engineered Exosome Packaging & Tropism**: Models packaging of therapeutic noncoding RNAs and CFTR-modulating payloads into club cell-derived apical EVs, evaluating recipient cell uptake across neighboring ciliated and basal cells.
4. **Council Registration**: Registers the new module in `DISCOVERY_MODULE_REGISTRY` in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py` with 100% anti-hallucination compliance.
