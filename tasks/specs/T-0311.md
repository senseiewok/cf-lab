## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical mRNA secondary structure visualizer on `src/cf/cftr/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/cftr/index.html`:
1. **CFTR Transcript Folding Landscape**: Visualizes thermodynamic minimum free energy ($\Delta G^\circ$, kcal/mol) RNA secondary structures, hairpin stem-loops, and putative G-quadruplex (G4) motifs across the NM_000492.4 coding sequence.
2. **Ribosome Transit Velocity & Pause Profiling**: Models translation elongation speed (codons/sec) as a function of local structural stability ($\Delta G < -15\,\text{kcal/mol}$) and rare codon clusters (e.g. at domain boundaries between NBD1, MSD2, and NBD2).
3. **Interactive Controls & Metrics**: Sliders for codon decoding rate ($5-20\,\text{codons/s}$), small-molecule G4 stabilizer / helicase ligand concentration, and mutation overlay for synonymous CFTR variants that alter mRNA folding.
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice medical boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_cftr_mrna_folding_explorer.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
