## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/cftr/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling thermodynamic competition between aberrant deep intronic splice-site decoy oligonucleotides and the human U1 snRNP spliceosomal complex:
1. **Splice Competitor Thermodynamics**: Simulates how synthetic U1 decoy oligonucleotides compete with human U1 snRNP binding to cryptic 5' splice donor motifs created by deep intronic CFTR mutations (3849+10kb C->T in intron 22, 1811+1.6kb A->G in intron 12).
2. **Pseudoexon Exclusion & Transcript Rescue**: Calculates the dose-dependent reduction in cryptic splice activation and corresponding restoration of full-length, in-frame CFTR mRNA transcript and functional protein expression.
3. **Off-Target Transcriptome Orthogonality**: Models base-pairing specificity and thermodynamic selectivity over native physiological 5' splice sites across the human transcriptome.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
