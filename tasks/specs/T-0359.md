## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling airway epithelial goblet cell metaplasia, IL-13/STAT6 signal transduction, and MUC5AC/MUC5B polymeric mucin secretion stoichiometry:
1. **Goblet vs Ciliated Cell Kinetic Balance**: Visualizes the proportional shift from normal pseudostratified ciliated epithelium (~5:1 ciliated-to-goblet ratio) to severe goblet cell hyperplasia (up to 1:1 or inverted ratios) triggered by TH2 cytokines and chronic infection.
2. **MUC5AC vs MUC5B Polymeric Viscoelasticity**: Dynamically computes the resulting mucus mesh hindrance, pore size contraction (from 200 nm down to < 30 nm), and effective yield stress under varying stoichiometric ratios of tethered/hypersecreted MUC5AC and baseline MUC5B.
3. **Delivery Flux Simulation**: Simulates the transport flux and penetration velocity of aerosolized lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) and extracellular vesicles (EVs) through normal vs metaplastic mucus gels.
4. **Accessible, Zero-Build Architecture**: Implements native ES modules with accessible SVG canvas, keyboard controls, ARIA landmark semantics, and strict dignity language boundaries.
