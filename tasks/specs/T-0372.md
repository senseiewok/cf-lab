## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling dry powder inhaler (DPI) aerosolization mechanics, carrier-lactose detachment fluid shear, and airway deposition:
1. **Carrier Detachment Biophysics**: Simulates adhesive van der Waals and electrostatic forces binding micronized CF therapeutics to coarse alpha-lactose monohydrate carrier crystals under airflow shear stress.
2. **Aerodynamic Lung Deposition Profiling**: Models Mass Median Aerodynamic Diameter (MMAD), Fine Particle Fraction (FPF < 5 um), and regional deposition fractions (oropharyngeal impaction, tracheobronchial sedimentation, and alveolar diffusion) across varying patient peak inspiratory flow rates (PIFR 30–90 L/min).
3. **Interactive Device Resistance Modeling**: Enables users to modulate inhaler airflow resistance, lactose surface roughness, and drug payload size to explore optimization for CF patients with compromised lung function (ppFEV1 < 40%).
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
