## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical ASL depth and hypertonic saline rehydration simulator on `src/cf/tree/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/tree/index.html`:
1. **ASL Osmotic Balance & Dehydration**: Models physiological ASL height (~7-10 $\mu\text{m}$) collapsing to depleted state (~2-3 $\mu\text{m}$) in CF due to absent CFTR-mediated chloride secretion and uninhibited ENaC sodium hyperabsorption.
2. **Hypertonic Saline (7% NaCl) Osmotic Rehydration**: Computes water flux ($J_v = L_p \cdot \Delta \Pi$) driven by apical osmotic gradient, transiently restoring ASL depth to 15-20 $\mu\text{m}$, enhancing ciliary beat frequency (CBF) and mucus clearance velocity ($MCV \approx 4-10\ \text{mm/min}$).
3. **Osmolyte Clearance & Half-life**: Models exponential dissipation of apical osmolarity through transepithelial NaCl diffusion and paracellular clearance ($t_{1/2} \approx 60-90\ \text{min}$).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_asl_osmotic_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
