## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive, accessible biophysical simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/ptc/` (with complete Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/ptc/`) demonstrating splice-site decoy competition and cryptic pseudoexon exclusion:
1. **Cryptic 5' and 3' Splice Site Selection**: Visualizes spliceosome assembly at deep intronic mutations (such as 3849+10kbC->T and 1811+1.6kbA->G), simulating U1 snRNP recognition and pseudoexon inclusion.
2. **U1 snRNP / ISS Decoy Oligonucleotide Competition**: Models competitive binding kinetics between endogenous U1 snRNA and modified U1 adaptor snRNAs or intronic splicing silencer (ISS) decoy oligonucleotides, demonstrating thermodynamic displacement of the spliceosome.
3. **Full-Length Mature CFTR Transcript Recovery**: Simulates dose-dependent recovery of canonical exon-exon junction formation, showing how decoy titration converts non-functional truncated transcripts into functional wild-type mRNA.
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Full WCAG 2.1 AAA accessible controls, slider inputs with live ARIA readout, colorblind-safe SVG/Canvas renderers, and zero external build dependencies.
