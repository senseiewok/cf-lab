## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/mrna_secondary_structure_model.py` in `cf-research` to calculate secondary structure folding free energies and elongation barrier kinetics across CFTR mRNA:
1. **Thermodynamic Free Energy Prediction**: Implements nearest-neighbor thermodynamic rules (Turner 2004 parameters) to evaluate stem-loop stability ($\Delta G^\circ_{37}$) across Exons 10, 11, and 12, encompassing the F508del (c.1521_1523delCTT) locus and adjacent codon contexts.
2. **Ribosome Translocation Barrier Kinetics**: Models ribosome elongation rates as a function of hairpin unwinding rate constants ($k_{\text{unwind}} = k_0 e^{-\Delta G^\ddagger / k_B T}$), predicting dwell times at specific codons.
3. **Synonymous Codon & ASO Rescue Predictions**: Evaluates how synonymous codon substitutions or steric-blocking antisense oligonucleotides alter hairpin stability and relieve translational stalling.
4. **Deterministic Validation**: Validates predictions against published SHAPE-MaP chemical probing datasets and registers self-tests.
