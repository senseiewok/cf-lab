## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/dpi_carrier_detachment_model.py` to model inhaled dry powder inhaler (DPI) formulations for CF drug delivery (e.g. inhaled mannitol, dry powder tobramycin, or lyophilized mRNA-LNP powders):
1. **Adhesion Thermodynamics & Rumpf/JKR Contact Mechanics**: Models cohesive and adhesive surface forces (van der Waals, electrostatic, and capillary meniscus forces) between micronized active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) particles (1-5 um) and coarse alpha-lactose monohydrate carrier crystals (50-100 um).
2. **Inhalation Flow Rate & Fluid Shear Detachment**: Simulates patient peak inspiratory flow (PIF 30-90 L/min) across device turbulence geometries, computing the critical aerodynamic shear stress and particle collision energy required for fine particle fraction (FPF) aerosolization.
3. **Deep Peripheral Lung Deposition Modeling**: Computes aerodynamic diameter distribution (MMAD), geometric standard deviation (GSD), and predicted bronchial vs alveolar deposition fractions under compromised CF inspiratory dynamics.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_dpi_carrier_detachment.py` and JSON schema validation for model exports.
