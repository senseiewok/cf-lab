## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/glycocalyx_charge_barrier_model.py` in `cf-research` to model electro-hydrodynamic filtration, steric mesh exclusion, and enzymatic permeabilization of the apical airway glycocalyx:
1. **Electrostatic Double-Layer & Charge Density Calculation**: Calculates Debye length ($\kappa^{-1} \approx 0.8\text{ nm}$ in airway surface liquid) and local electrostatic potential as a function of sialic acid and glycosaminoglycan surface charge density ($\sigma \approx -0.02\text{ to }-0.05\text{ C/m}^2$).
2. **Hindered Transport & Steric Partitioning**: Evaluates the Renkin-Ogston steric partitioning coefficient $\Phi = (1 - r_p / r_f)^2$ for nanoparticle radii $r_p \in [10, 100]\text{ nm}$ moving through fibrous glycocalyx networks of effective fiber radius $r_f$.
3. **Enzymatic Desialylation Kinetics**: Models Michaelis-Menten desialylation kinetics of bacterial and recombinant human neuraminidase/sialidases (Neu1, Neu2, Neu3), calculating the time required to reduce negative surface charge by $80\%$ and increase particle diffusivity by $> 5$-fold.
4. **Deterministic Validation**: Compares model predictions against published airway epithelial micro-rheology and nanoparticle tracking analysis (NTA) datasets, providing `--check` self-tests.
