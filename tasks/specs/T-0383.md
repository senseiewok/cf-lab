## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling the competitive kinetic partitioning of inhaled therapeutic nanoparticles between mucociliary advective clearance and alveolar/airway macrophage phagocytosis:
1. **Kinetic Partitioning Phase Space**: Visualizes how nanoparticle hydrodynamic diameter ($d_{\text{np}} = 50\text{--}500\text{ nm}$), surface PEGylation density ($\Gamma_{\text{PEG}}$), and zeta potential ($\zeta = -30\text{ to }+30\text{ mV}$) determine the fraction of particles translocating across the airway epithelial barrier versus cleared.
2. **Mucociliary vs Phagocytic Flux Competition**: Dynamically computes advective mucus clearance ($v_{\text{mcc}} = 1\text{--}5\text{ mm/min}$) against size-dependent macrophage engulfment kinetics ($k_{\text{phag}}$).
3. **Interactive Vector Design Optimization**: Provides interactive sliders for mucus mesh pore cutoff, macrophage scavenger receptor binding affinity ($K_D$), and aerosol deposition depth, allowing researchers and patients to explore non-viral CFTR delivery windows.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
