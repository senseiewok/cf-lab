## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/ptc_erf_depletion_readthrough.py` modeling aminoglycoside-free translational readthrough of CFTR premature termination codons:
1. **Competitive Ribosomal A-Site Kinetics**: Implements kinetic competition between eRF1-eRF3-GTP termination complex and near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs at premature stop codons.
2. **eRF Depletion Dynamics**: Simulates small-molecule termination inhibitor cooperativity ($IC_{50} \in [0.1, 1.0]\ \mu\text{M}$, $h \in [1.5, 2.0]$) conferring a 5- to 15-fold readthrough boost upon $60\text{--}80\%$ release factor depletion.
3. **Codon Hierarchy & Context Factor**: Models termination permissiveness ($\text{UGA} > \text{UAG} \gg \text{UAA}$) and +4 flanking nucleotide context weighting ($\omega_{\text{context}} \approx 0.35$ for +4 C/U in W1282X vs $1.0$ for +4 G).
4. **Coupled NMD Escape & Mature Band C Expression**: Integrates transcript stabilization from nonsense-mediated decay with translation elongation to calculate functional 170 kDa mature CFTR Band C output.
