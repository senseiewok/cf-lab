## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical ASL hydration visualizer on `src/cf/tree/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/tree/index.html`:
1. **Osmotic Water Flux Kinetics**: Implements the Starling osmotic flux equation $J_v = L_p (\Delta P - \sigma \Delta \Pi)$ where $\Delta \Pi = R T \sum \Delta C_i$, modeling apical water movement across the epithelial barrier following nebulization of 3% vs. 7% hypertonic saline.
2. **Periciliary Liquid (PCL) Layer Height Recovery**: Models dynamic restoration of the PCL layer from collapsed state ($<3\,\mu\text{m}$) up to physiological clearance height ($7\pm1\,\mu\text{m}$) and tracks ciliary beat frequency recovery.
3. **Interactive Controls & Metrics**: Sliders for saline concentration (0.9% isotonic, 3%, 7%), nebulization dose ($0.5-4\,\text{mL}$), and baseline ENaC sodium hyperabsorption rate ($1.0-3.5\times$).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice medical boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_asl_osmotic_hydration_explorer.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
