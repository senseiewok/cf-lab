## 1. Curative Mission & Discovery Science Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/lnp_escape_dynamics.py` to model the intracellular trafficking, endosomal acidification, and cytosolic cargo release kinetics of lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) in primary CF airway basal stem cells ($KRT5^+ / TP63^+$). Simulates proton-sponge buffering and ionizable lipid $pK_a$ inversion (physiological $pK_a = 6.2\text{--}6.8$), endosomal membrane destabilization via inverted hexagonal ($\text{H}_{\text{II}}$) phase transitions, and galectin-3 ($LGALS3$) endosomal rupture tracking. Quantifies cytosolic intact mRNA/DNA bioavailability fraction ($\phi_{\text{escape}} = 1\text{--}5\%$) and translation onset half-life ($t_{1/2}$) to guide chemical formulation design for long-term stem cell correction.
