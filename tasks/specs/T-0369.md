## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/submucosal_gland_fluid_model.py` to model human airway submucosal gland serous acinar cell primary fluid secretion, transcellular osmotic water flow mediated by Aquaporin-5 (AQP5), and synergistic CFTR/CaCC transepithelial electrochemistry:
1. **Osmotic Coupling & Transepithelial Water Flux**: Couples apical chloride (CFTR + TMEM16A/ANO1) and basolateral potassium/sodium/chloride cotransport (NKCC1, KCNQ1/KCNE3) to compute the transcellular transepithelial osmotic gradient and AQP5-mediated hydraulic conductivity.
2. **Submucosal Gland Duct Viscous Obstruction**: Models ductal transit, bicarbonate-dependent mucin untangling (SLC26A4 pendrin / CFTR HCO3- transport), and protein hyperconcentration in CFTR-null acini leading to ductal plugging.
3. **Agonist-Induced Hypersecretion**: Simulates cholinergic (carbachol / VIP) vs beta-adrenergic secretory drives and computes volumetric secretion rate (nL/min/gland) under wild-type, modulator-treated, and gene-corrected states.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_submucosal_gland_fluid.py` and JSON schema validation for model exports.
