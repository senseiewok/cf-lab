## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/ptc/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling human CFTR mRNA secondary structure folding, local minimum free energy (MFE) pseudoknot landscapes, and codon-specific ribosome elongation velocity:
1. **Secondary Structure & Ribosome Pausing**: Simulates mRNA stem-loop and hairpin formation ($\Delta G^\circ_{\text{fold}}$ calculated via nearest-neighbor thermodynamic parameters) and correlates high-stability secondary structures with ribosome dwell time and translational stalling.
2. **Premature Termination Codon (PTC) Readthrough Context**: Explores how local mRNA secondary structures adjacent to premature stop codons (G542X, W1282X, R553X) modulate aminoglycoside/eRF1 competition, stop-codon context (+4 base), and nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) UPF1 surveillance triggering.
3. **Interactive Optimization Controls**: Sliders for GC-content window, local folding stability, codon adaptation index (CAI), and potentiator/readthrough compound concentration, demonstrating synthetic mRNA stabilization strategies.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
