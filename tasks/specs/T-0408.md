## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/aerosol_droplet_deposition_mechanics.py` modeling aerosol fluid dynamics and tracheobronchial deposition of inhaled gene therapies (mRNA LNPs and viral vectors):
1. **Multi-Compartment Airway Deposition Fractions**: Implements Weibel symmetric lung morphology (generations 0 to 16) modeling inertial impaction in upper conducting airways ($G_0\text{--}G_6$), gravitational sedimentation in small conducting bronchioles ($G_7\text{--}G_{12}$), and Brownian diffusion in terminal bronchioles ($G_{13}\text{--}G_{16}$).
2. **Log-Normal Droplet Aerodynamic Sizing**: Analyzes droplet Mass Median Aerodynamic Diameter ($\text{MMAD} \in [1.0, 5.0]\ \mu\text{m}$) and Geometric Standard Deviation ($\text{GSD} \in [1.5, 2.5]$) to maximize target bronchial deposition while minimizing alveolar dissipation and oropharyngeal loss.
3. **Inspiratory Flow & Airway Narrowing Impact**: Simulates deposition shifts under CF pathological airway narrowing (luminal plugging, bronchiectasis) across variable inspiratory flow rates ($Q_{\text{insp}} \in [15, 60]\ \text{L/min}$).
4. **Targeted Delivery Fraction Metric**: Derives the Bronchial Delivery Index ($\text{BDI} = \text{Fraction}_{\text{bronchial}} / \text{Fraction}_{\text{total}}$) benchmarking delivery platforms.
