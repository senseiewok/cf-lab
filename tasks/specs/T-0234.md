# T-0234: Extracellular Vesicle (EV) Basal Stem Cell Tropism & Trans-Tight-Junction Permeation Model

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track A)
Airway basal stem cells ($KRT5^+$, $TP63^+$) reside beneath a pseudostratified layer of columnar ciliated and secretory cells sealed by apical junctional complexes (tight junctions: ZO-1, Claudins; adherens junctions: E-cadherin). Intact tight junctions create a severe steric barrier ($R_{\text{pore}} < 2\text{ nm}$) that blocks apical gene delivery vehicles (AAVs, LNPs) from reaching the basal niche.

Engineered extracellular vesicles (EVs / exosomes; Ivleva et al., *Front. Bioeng. Biotechnol.* 2026, DOI `10.3389/fbioe.2026.1855907`) exhibit natural membrane fluidity, intrinsic tetraspanin-mediated cell fusion (CD9, CD63, CD81), and can be pseudotyped with basolateral-targeting ligands (e.g. integrin $\alpha_6\beta_4$ binders or vesicular stomatitis virus G glycoprotein) to exploit transient paracellular or receptor-mediated transcytotic pathways into basal progenitors.

This specification directs the creation of a computational transport model in `cf-research` (`tools/discovery/ev_basal_tropism.py`) parameterizing EV size, surface pseudotyping, tight junction permeation kinetics under mild perturbation, and basal stem cell transduction efficiency.

## 3. Mathematical & Kinetic Model
1. **Trans-Tight-Junction Paracellular Permeation**:
   $$J_{\text{para}} = P_{\text{TJ}}(t) \cdot \left(C_{\text{apical}} - C_{\text{basolateral}}\right)$$
   Where the effective tight-junction permeability $P_{\text{TJ}}(t)$ incorporates baseline resistance ($R_{\text{TEER}} \approx 800–1200\ \Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$) and transient acoustic or osmotic loosening:
   $$P_{\text{TJ}}(t) = P_{\text{baseline}} \cdot \left(1 + \beta \cdot e^{-t / \tau_{\text{recovery}}}\right)$$
2. **Basal Stem Cell Endocytic & Fusion Kinetics**:
   $$v_{\text{entry}} = V_{\max} \cdot \frac{C_{\text{basolateral}}}{K_D + C_{\text{basolateral}}} \cdot \Phi_{\text{pseudotype}}$$
   Where $\Phi_{\text{pseudotype}}$ is the surface ligand enhancement factor ($\ge 1.0$) for CD9/integrin-directed EVs.
3. **Chimeric Stem Cell Correction Accumulation**:
   $$\frac{dF_{\text{basal}}}{dt} = \eta_{\text{payload}} \cdot v_{\text{entry}} \cdot \left(1 - F_{\text{basal}}\right) - \mu_{\text{clearance}} F_{\text{basal}}$$
   Reaching steady-state stem cell chimerism $F_{\text{basal}}^*$, which sustains permanent lifelong wild-type CFTR expression across airway lineages.
