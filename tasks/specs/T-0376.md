## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/cftr_chaperone_ubiquitin_model.py` to model the kinetic triage and competitive binding partitioning between molecular chaperones (Hsp70, Hsc70, Hsp90) that facilitate CFTR nucleotide-binding domain 1 (NBD1) folding vs E3 ubiquitin ligases (CHIP / STUB1, RMA1 / RNF5) that target misfolded Delta-F508 CFTR for proteasomal degradation:
1. **Kinetic Partitioning Equations**: Models time-dependent association/dissociation of Hsp70 and co-chaperones (Hdj-2/DNAJA1) on nascent CFTR polypeptide chains.
2. **CHIP Ubiquitination Threshold**: Simulates how delayed NBD1 folding kinetics prolong chaperone dwell time, triggering CHIP TPR-domain binding and polyubiquitination via UbcH5a.
3. **Pharmacological Chaperone Synergy**: Evaluates how correctors (lumacaftor, tezacaftor, elexacaftor) alter folding free energy landscapes to bias kinetic partitioning toward ER export rather than ERAD destruction.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_cftr_chaperone_ubiquitin_model.py` and registers check rows in `check-all.ps1`.
