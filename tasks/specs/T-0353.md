## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive biophysical simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/delivery/`) modeling airway epithelial cell composition, ciliary beat dynamics, and mucociliary transport velocity:
1. **Ciliated to Goblet Cell Ratio**: Models healthy physiological epithelia (~5:1 to 8:1 ciliated-to-goblet cell ratio) versus CF chronic airway remodeling and goblet cell hyperplasia (< 2:1 ratio).
2. **Ciliary Beat Frequency & Metachronal Coordination**: Simulates coordinated ciliary beat frequency (CBF, 10–15 Hz) in healthy periciliary liquid (PCL, 7 μm height) versus viscous collapse and ciliary immobility in depleted PCL (< 3 μm).
3. **Escalator Clearance Velocity**: Computes effective mucociliary clearance transport velocity (mm/min) across airway generations (trachea, bronchi, bronchioles) and illustrates how mucolytics and CFTR restoration restore clearance.
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Native zero-build Web Components, live ARIA accessibility announcements, reduced-motion compliance, and colorblind-safe SVG/Canvas renderers.
