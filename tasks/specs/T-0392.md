## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and comprehensive unit test suite in `tools/discovery/tmem16a_calcium_conductance_model.py` evaluating TMEM16A (ANO1) calcium-activated chloride channel (CaCC) conductance:
1. **Dual Calcium Cooperativity & Voltage Gating**: Implements Hill-equation open probability ($P_o$) with Hill coefficient $n_H \approx 2.0$ and voltage-dependent calcium sensitivity shifts ($z_{\text{eff}} \approx 0.40$).
2. **Allosteric Potentiator Left-Shifting**: Models small-molecule potentiators (e.g., ETD002) shifting apparent calcium affinity ($K_{0.5}$) into resting basal range ($100\text{--}200\ \text{nM}$).
3. **Transepithelial Secretion & ASL Hydration**: Calculates apical chloride flux ($I_{\text{Cl}}$) and osmotic fluid replenishment to sustain periciliary liquid layer height $\ge 7\ \mu\text{m}$.
