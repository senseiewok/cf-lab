## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops a mechanistic Python biophysical simulation in `tools/discovery/asl_osmotic_restoration_model.py`:
1. **Transepithelial Water Movement Dynamics**: Solves time-dependent differential equations for apical osmotic equilibrium following hypertonic saline deposition:
   $$\frac{dh_{\text{PCL}}}{dt} = L_p \left[ \sigma_{\text{NaCl}} R T \left( C_{\text{apical}} - C_{\text{submucosal}} \right) - P_{\text{hydro}} \right] - J_{\text{ciliary\_drain}}$$
2. **Sodium Absorption & ENaC Clearance**: Models rapid active absorption of deposited $\text{Na}^+$ ions through apical ENaC channels ($V_{\max} = 15\,\mu\text{mol}/(\text{cm}^2\cdot\text{h})$, $K_m = 40\,\text{mM}$) and basolateral $\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+$-ATPase.
3. **Mucal Viscoelasticity Coupling**: Calculates resultant decreases in mucus percent solids (from $8-10\%$ solids down to physiological $2-3\%$) and mucus shear storage modulus $G'$.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive test suite in `tools/discovery/test_asl_osmotic_restoration_model.py` and anti-hallucination validation.
