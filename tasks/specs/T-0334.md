## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops a biophysical simulation tool `tools/discovery/ptc_termination_flux_model.py` and unit test suite `tools/discovery/test_ptc_termination_flux_model.py` for mathematical modeling of ribosome stop codon decoding and readthrough efficiency:
1. **Kinetic Competition Master Equation**: Models rates of termination $k_{\text{term}}$ (eRF1-eRF3 recognition and peptide release) versus near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA accommodation $k_{\text{read}}$.
2. **Context-Dependent Codon Matrix**: Computes readthrough probability across stop codon identities ($UGA > UAG > UAA$) and +4 through +6 nucleotide context flanking CFTR mutations G542X, R553X, R1162X, and W1282X.
3. **Pharmacological Modulation & Functional Recovery**: Simulates concentration-dependent readthrough stimulation by aminoglycosides (G418, gentamicin) and non-aminoglycosides (ELX-02, ataluren), predicting restored mature Band C CFTR protein fraction.
4. **Anti-Hallucination & Discovery Registry**: Registers module in `discovery_council.py`, integrates with `tools/check_anti_hallucination.py` (AH-1..AH-5), and outputs deterministic JSON results.
