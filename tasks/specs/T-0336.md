## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive pre-mRNA splicing regulatory kinetics simulator on `src/cf/cftr/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/cftr/index.html`:
1. **Cryptic Exon Splicing Competition**: Models competitive binding between U1 snRNP at cryptic 5' splice donor sites versus engineered intronic splicing silencer (ISS) decoys in deep intronic CFTR mutations (e.g. `3849+10kbC->T`, `1811+1.6kbA->G`).
2. **Thermodynamic Free-Energy Landscape**: Computes thermodynamic hybridization free energies ($\Delta G^\circ$) and competitive binding isotherms determining the fraction of full-length correctly spliced CFTR transcript versus pseudoexon inclusion.
3. **Restoration Dose-Response**: Simulates phenotypic CFTR mature Band C rescue and apical chloride current ($I_{\text{sc}}$) restoration as a function of decoy ASO concentration ($K_d \approx 1-10\ \text{nM}$).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_u1_snrnp_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
