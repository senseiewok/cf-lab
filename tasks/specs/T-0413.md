## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/nebulizer_shear_stress_integrity.py` modeling hydrodynamic shear stress and mechanical stability of aerosolized CFTR mRNA lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) during nebulizer passage:
1. **Pore Extrusion Shear Stress**: Formulates Hagen-Poiseuille shear stress $\tau_{\text{wall}} = \frac{4 \mu Q}{\pi r_{\text{pore}}^3}$ across micro-aperture mesh nozzles ($r_{\text{pore}} \in [1.5, 4.0]\ \mu\text{m}$) and vibrating mesh frequencies ($f \in [100, 180]\ \text{kHz}$).
2. **LNP Vesicle Deformation & Rupture**: Evaluates critical capillary number $\text{Ca}_{\text{crit}} = \frac{\tau r_{\text{lnp}}}{\sigma_{\text{bilayer}}}$ and membrane bending rigidity $\kappa_b \approx 10\text{--}20\ k_B T$, determining fractional mRNA leakage under high-velocity extrusion.
3. **Post-Aerosolization Colloid Aggregation**: Simulates secondary aggregate formation and hydrodynamic diameter shift ($\Delta d_h$) competing with mucociliary clearance in airway mucus.
4. **Platform Mechanical Benchmark**: Benchmarks vibrating mesh nebulizers against ultrasonic and pneumatic jet nebulizers for intact mRNA payload delivery.
