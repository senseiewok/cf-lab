## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/atp_catalytic_water_model.py` to simulate the asymmetric nucleotide-binding domain dimer (NBD1 degenerate site vs NBD2 catalytic site) ATP hydrolysis gating cycle and catalytic water coordination thermodynamics:
1. **Asymmetric Dimer Catalytic Pocket Formulation**: Models ATP binding free energies at Site 1 (NBD1 degenerate site: non-hydrolytic, tightly bound ATP) versus Site 2 (NBD2 catalytic site: rapid ATP hydrolysis driving channel closing).
2. **Catalytic Water Coordination Energy Barrier**: Simulates attack of catalytic water on the ATP $\gamma$-phosphate, calculating the activation energy barrier ($\Delta G^\ddagger \sim 18.5\text{ kcal/mol}$) and electrostatic stabilization by Gln1291, Glu1371, and His1402.
3. **Allosteric Coupling to Pore Open Probability ($P_o$)**: Connects NBD dimer closed-head conformation stability to pore-opening transition states across TM6 and TM12 helices, predicting open probability changes under potentiator (ivacaftor, deutivacaftor) modulation.
4. **Council Registration**: Registers the new module in `DISCOVERY_MODULE_REGISTRY` in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py` with 100% anti-hallucination compliance.
