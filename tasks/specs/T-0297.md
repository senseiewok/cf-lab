## 1. Curative Mission & Discovery Scope

Delivers `tools/discovery/macrophage_scavenger_kinetics.py` and unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_macrophage_scavenger_kinetics.py` to quantitatively model receptor-mediated clearance of aerosolized non-viral genetic delivery vehicles by human pulmonary alveolar macrophages. Evaluates competitive ligand binding across scavenger receptors (SR-A1, CD36, MARCO) vs. surface passivation density (PEG-PLGA, zwitterionic poly(carboxybetaine), CD47 "Self" peptide). Computes phagocytic velocity $V_{\text{uptake}}$, receptor saturation constants ($K_d$), and projected retention half-life ($t_{1/2}$) in the cystic fibrosis airway lumen. Registers module in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py`.
