## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#enac-tmem16a-explorer` on `/cf/tree/` to simulate coupled transepithelial ion transport across human airway epithelia. Couples **Epithelial Sodium Channel (ENaC)** sodium absorption with **TMEM16A / ANO1** calcium-activated chloride conductance and CFTR apical chloride secretion. Simulates Ussing chamber short-circuit current dynamics ($I_{\text{sc}}$), transepithelial potential difference ($V_{\text{te}}$), and airway surface liquid (ASL) periciliary layer depth ($\mu\text{m}$) across healthy, CF-untreated, potentiated, and dual-inhibition therapeutic regimens. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
