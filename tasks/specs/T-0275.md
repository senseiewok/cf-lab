## 1. Curative Mission & User Value

Translates the newly verified CF airway mucus micro-rheology and polymer cleavage kinetics model (`T-0253`, `mucus_cleavage_kinetics.py`) into an interactive bilingual (EN / `es-MX`) simulation card on `/cf/delivery/`. Visualizes the coupled enzymatic degradation of the purulent eDNA-mucin hydrogel network under rhDNase (dornase alfa) with G-actin competitive inhibition ($K_i = 15.0$ nM; `PDB:1ATN`, `PDB:1DNK`, `PDB:4AWN`) alongside thiol-reducing disulfide cleavage of MUC5B/MUC5AC by N-acetylcysteine (NAC). Couples dynamic pore network expansion with De Gennes and Ogston hindered diffusion models to calculate effective permeation rates for AAV ($r = 13$ nm) and LNP ($r = 40$ nm) delivery payloads.
