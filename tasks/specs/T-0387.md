## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a biophysical mathematical model in `tools/discovery/glycocalyx_charge_barrier_model.py` quantifying the negative electrostatic charge density and steric exclusion generated by terminal sialic acid residues on airway epithelial cell glycocalyx:
1. **Transmembrane Mucin Sialylation Density**: Formulates negative charge density profiles generated by $\alpha$-2,3 and $\alpha$-2,6 sialic acid linkages on transmembrane mucins MUC1, MUC4, and MUC16 as a function of distance from the apical plasma membrane ($z = 0\text{--}500\text{ nm}$).
2. **Poisson-Boltzmann Electrostatic Potential**: Computes electrostatic potential $\psi(z)$ and Debye screening lengths across physiological airway ionic strengths ($I = 50\text{--}250\text{ mM}$).
3. **Partitioning & Permeation Flux**: Calculates the thermodynamic partition coefficient and effective diffusivity of anionic vs. PEGylated vs. cationic gene delivery vectors, providing quantitative rationale for sialidase-mediated permeabilization protocols.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive test suite in `tools/discovery/test_glycocalyx_charge_barrier_model.py`.
