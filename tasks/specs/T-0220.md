# T-0220: Airway Basal Stem Cell (KRT5+/TP63+) Niche Turnover & Vector Persistence Kinetics Model

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track A)
A durable cure for Cystic Fibrosis via in vivo gene therapy requires transducing long-lived airway progenitor cells. Differentiated airway epithelia (ciliated cells, club cells, ionocytes) naturally turn over with a half-life of 90–120 days. Non-integrating modalities (e.g. mRNA-LNPs such as `VX-522` or episomal AAV vectors such as `4D-710`) suffer inevitable dilution or loss as differentiated cells shed, whereas vectors transducing the submucosal and pseudostratified basal stem cell niche (`KRT5+`, `TP63+`) can sustain phenotypic correction through clonal self-renewal.

This specification directs the creation of a mathematical persistence model in `cf-research` modeling the kinetics of basal stem cell self-renewal versus differentiation, contrasting episomal vs integrative CFTR gene addition across long-term clinical horizons (1 to 10 years).

## 2. Primary Evidence Grounding
- **Airway Epithelial Census & Stem Cell Hierarchy**: Montoro et al., "A revised airway epithelial hierarchy includes CFTR-expressing ionocytes", *Nature*, 2018. DOI: `10.1038/s41586-018-0394-6`.
- **In Vivo Aerosolized mRNA Delivery**: Phase 1/2 clinical study of aerosolized LNP-formulated mRNA for CFTR supplementation. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT05248230`.
- **Aerosolized Targeted Capsid Gene Delivery**: Clinical evaluation of aerosolized recombinant AAV vector targeting lung airway epithelia. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT06515002`.

## 3. Mathematical & Kinetic Model
The airway epithelial architecture is modeled as a multi-compartment dynamical system:
$$\frac{dB(t)}{dt} = \lambda_B (2p_s - 1) B(t)$$
$$\frac{dT(t)}{dt} = 2\lambda_B (1 - p_s) B(t) + \lambda_T (2q_s - 1) T(t) - \gamma_T T(t)$$
$$\frac{dD(t)}{dt} = \gamma_T T(t) - \mu_D D(t)$$

Where:
- $B(t)$: Basal stem cell pool density ($KRT5^+$, $TP63^+$). Under homeostatic equilibrium, symmetric division probability $p_s = 0.5$, ensuring constant stem pool size $B_0$.
- $T(t)$: Transit-amplifying intermediate progenitor pool ($KRT4^+$, $KRT13^+$).
- $D(t)$: Terminally differentiated functional luminal epithelia ($FOXJ1^+$ ciliated, $MUC5AC^+$ secretory, $FOXI1^+$ ionocytes) with turnover rate $\mu_D \approx \ln(2)/100\text{ days}^{-1}$.
- Vector retention kinetics:
  1. **Episomal Vector (mRNA / Non-integrating DNA)**:
     $$C_{\text{episomal}}(t) = C_0 \cdot \exp(-\mu_D t) + C_{\text{basal},0} \cdot 2^{-k_{\text{div}} t} \cdot \exp(-\delta_{\text{nuclease}} t)$$
  2. **Integrative / Gene Editing Correction**:
     $$F_{\text{corrected}}(t) = \frac{B_{\text{edited}}}{B_0} \cdot \left(1 - \exp(-\mu_D t)\right) + F_{\text{diff},0} \cdot \exp(-\mu_D t)$$
     As $t \to \infty$, $F_{\text{corrected}}(t) \to \frac{B_{\text{edited}}}{B_0}$, maintaining permanent homeostatic CFTR ion conductance.
