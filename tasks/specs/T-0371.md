## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/tree/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling human airway submucosal gland serous acinar primary fluid secretion, Aquaporin-5 (AQP5) transcellular water flux, and CFTR/TMEM16A chloride electrochemistry:
1. **Acinar Fluid Secretion Dynamics**: Simulates basolateral Na-K-2Cl cotransporter (NKCC1) chloride uptake, apical CFTR / CaCC anion conductance, and AQP5-mediated transcellular water movement driven by osmotic gradients.
2. **Pathological Mucus Viscosity Inversion**: Visualizes how absent or defective CFTR in submucosal glands causes severe hyposecretion and hyper-concentrated, protein-dense mucus plugs that obstruct gland ducts before emerging into the airway lumen.
3. **Interactive Pharmacological Modulation**: Provides real-time sliders for CFTR potentiator efficacy, cholinergic/muscarinic receptor activation, and transepithelial osmotic gradient to evaluate restoration of fluid clearance.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
