## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Deploys an interactive ribosomal PTC suppression and toxicity simulator on `src/cf/ptc/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/ptc/index.html`:
1. **Codon-Specific Readthrough Kinetics**: Models differential near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA mispairing probabilities across UGA ($3\times$ higher baseline readthrough), UAG, and UAA stop codons in the presence of readthrough agents (G418, gentamicin, ELX-02).
2. **Ribosomal Decoding Center Decoding**: Visualizes aminoglycoside binding within the decoding A-site of the 16S/18S rRNA helix 44 and allosteric conformational locking of adenines A1492/A1493.
3. **Therapeutic Window vs. Toxicity Trade-off**: Interactive curves mapping CFTR protein rescue percentage (0-15% of wild-type) against renal proximal tubule accumulation and cochlear hair cell ototoxicity thresholds.
4. **Bilingual Parity & Accessibility**: Semantic HTML5 controls, ARIA live metrics, Mexican Spanish translation parity, and dignity boundary safeguards.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_ptc_readthrough_kinetics_explorer.py` and anti-hallucination validation.
