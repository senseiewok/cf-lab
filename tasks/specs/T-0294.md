## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#glycocalyx-permeabilization-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate the dense apical glycocalyx meshwork ($H \approx 400\text{ nm}$) of human ciliated airway epithelial cells as a steric and electrostatic barrier to genetic vector penetration. Implements Stokes-Einstein mesh penetration kinetics, terminal sialic acid negative charge electrostatic repulsion ($\psi \approx -35\text{ mV}$), and transient enzymatic desialylation / sialidase treatment to enhance apical membrane docking. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
