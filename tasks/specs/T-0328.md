## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops a biophysical ribosome decoding model in `tools/discovery/ptc_ribosome_readthrough_model.py` simulating competitive translation kinetics at premature termination codons in CFTR:
1. **Competitive Decoding Kinetics**: Models stochastic competition between near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA mispairing ($k_{\text{read}}$) and eukaryotic release factor complex binding ($k_{\text{term}}$, eRF1-eRF3-GTP) at premature stop codons.
2. **Context-Dependent Allele Stratification**: Evaluates readthrough efficiency across key CF nonsense mutations:
   - W1282X (UGA +4 C): high basal mispairing propensity, elevated readthrough responsiveness
   - G542X (UGA +4 A): intermediate readthrough efficiency
   - R553X (UGA +4 U): moderate readthrough efficiency
   - R1162X (UGA +4 G): context-suppressed readthrough
3. **Synergy Coefficient Computation**: Computes combination index ($CI$) and Loewe additivity envelopes for dual-agent regimens pairing aminoglycoside mimetics (ELX-02) with eRF1/GSPT1 degraders (CC-90009).
4. **Deterministic Verification**: Comprehensive unit test suite in `tools/discovery/test_ptc_ribosome_readthrough_model.py` with 100% parameter bounds validation.
