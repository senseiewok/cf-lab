# T-0221: CFTR Potentiator + Triple-Corrector Cooperative Allosteric Thermodynamic Cycle

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track A)
Combination CFTR modulator therapies—such as Trikafta (`VX-445` / `VX-661` / `VX-770`) and next-generation once-daily regimens (`VX-121` / `VX-661` / `VX-561`)—exhibit profound synergy beyond simple additive folding or gating rescue. Cryo-EM structural determinations have revealed distinct allosteric binding pockets:
1. Type I Correctors (`VX-661` / Tezacaftor): Bind within the transmembrane domain 1 (TMD1) pocket, stabilizing the NBD1-TMD interface.
2. Type III Correctors (`VX-445` / Elexacaftor or `VX-121` / Vanzacaftor): Bind to transmembrane domain 2 (TMD2) and the regulatory extension, functioning as both a folding chaperone and allosteric potentiator.
3. Potentiators (`VX-770` / Ivacaftor or `VX-561` / Deutivacaftor): Bind to the lipid-facing TMD hinge (TM4/TM5/TM8) to bias gating toward the open ion conduction state.

This specification directs the implementation of a thermodynamic linkage and allosteric gating cycle in `cf-research` that models the energetic cooperativity ($\Delta\Delta G$) of simultaneous corrector and potentiator binding.

## 3. Mathematical & Biophysical Model
The channel exists in equilibrium between closed ($C$) and open ($O$) states, governed by the unliganded equilibrium constant $L_0 = \frac{[C_0]}{[O_0]} = \exp\left(\frac{\Delta G_0}{RT}\right)$.
For $N$ distinct allosteric ligands (e.g. Corrector 1, Corrector 2, Potentiator) with binding dissociation constants $K_{d,i}^C$ (closed state) and $K_{d,i}^O$ (open state):

$$\alpha_i = \frac{K_{d,i}^C}{K_{d,i}^O} = \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta\Delta G_{\text{open},i}}{RT}\right)$$

When $\alpha_i > 1$, ligand $i$ preferentially stabilizes the open pore state ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{open},i} < 0$).
Taking into account pairwise structural coupling cooperativity parameters $\gamma_{ij}$ between distinct pockets:

$$P_{\text{open}}([L_1], [L_2], [L_3]) = \frac{\prod_{i} \left(1 + \frac{[L_i]}{K_{d,i}^O}\right) \cdot \prod_{i < j} \gamma_{ij}^O}{\prod_{i} \left(1 + \frac{[L_i]}{K_{d,i}^O}\right) \prod_{i < j} \gamma_{ij}^O + L_0 \prod_{i} \left(1 + \frac{[L_i]}{K_{d,i}^C}\right) \prod_{i < j} \gamma_{ij}^C}$$

The model calculates:
- Open channel probability $P_{\text{open}}$ as a function of concentrations $[L_i]$.
- Single-channel current $I = \gamma_{\text{Cl}} \cdot P_{\text{open}} \cdot (V_m - E_{\text{Cl}})$.
- Thermodynamic synergy metric $S_{123} = \frac{P_{\text{open}}(1,2,3) - P_{\text{open}}(0)}{\sum_i [P_{\text{open}}(i) - P_{\text{open}}(0)]}$.
