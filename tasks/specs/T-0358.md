## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/ptc_readthrough_fidelity_model.py` to quantitatively model near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA misincorporation probabilities and resulting CFTR functional channel rescue:
1. **Ribosomal Decoding Center Kinetics**: Models the kinetic competition between eukaryotic release factors (eRF1-eRF3) and near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs at premature stop codons (UGA for W1282X and G542X, UAA for R553X, UAG for R1162X) under drug perturbation.
2. **Amino Acid Insertion Landscape**: Computes the insertion frequency of tryptophan, arginine, cysteine, or serine at position 1282 / 542 and the corresponding single-channel open probability (Po) and gating cooperativity of each resulting mutant CFTR protein.
3. **NMD Evasion & Translation Velocity**: Integrates with upstream nonsense-mediated decay kinetics to predict net functional apical chloride conductance rescue (% of wild-type).
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests, JSON export, and schema verification in `tools/discovery/test_ptc_readthrough_fidelity_model.py`.
