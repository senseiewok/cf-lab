## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling bacteriophage depolymerase enzyme kinetics and antibiotic synergy against mucoid Pseudomonas aeruginosa biofilms:
1. **Exopolysaccharide (EPS) Hydrolysis Kinetics**: Models tailspike alginate lyase degradation of poly-beta-D-mannuronate and alpha-L-guluronate matrix, quantifying reduction in microcolony thickness and matrix viscosity.
2. **Antibiotic Diffusive Permeability Enhancement**: Computes increased diffusion coefficient of inhaled tobramycin across enzyme-cleared biofilm pores ($D_{\text{eff}} / D_0$ increasing 5- to 20-fold).
3. **Synergistic Bacterial Eradication**: Simulates combined bacteriophage lytic cycles and aminoglycoside bactericidal killing curves under microaerophilic conditions.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) dictionary parity in `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js`.
