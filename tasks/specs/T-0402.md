## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/phage_depolymerase_biofilm_synergy.py` modeling bacteriophage alginate depolymerase enzymatic degradation of mucoid *P. aeruginosa* biofilms and antibiotic synergy:
1. **Enzymatic Matrix Depolymerization**: Implements Michaelis-Menten cleavage kinetics of O-acetylated alginate ($k_{\text{cat}} / K_m \in [10^4, 10^6]\ \text{M}^{-1}\text{s}^{-1}$) by phage-derived polysaccharide lyases.
2. **Diffusivity Recovery Dynamics**: Models the dynamic recovery of effective tobramycin diffusivity from $D_{\text{eff}} / D_0 < 0.05$ up to $D_{\text{eff}} / D_0 \ge 0.70$ upon matrix de-densification.
3. **Spatiotemporal Reaction-Diffusion**: Simulates antibiotic penetration through biofilm thickness ($z \ge 100\ \mu\text{m}$) accounting for reversible binding and bacterial uptake.
4. **Bactericidal Synergy Quantification**: Calculates combined log-reduction kill kinetics, demonstrating a synergistic reduction $\ge 3.0\ \log_{10}\text{ CFU}$ for phage depolymerase + tobramycin over monotherapy.
