## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive, accessible biophysical simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/cftr/` (with complete Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/cftr/`) demonstrating CFTR mRNA secondary structure dynamics:
1. **Stem-Loop Folding Free Energy Landscape**: Visualizes predicted secondary structures (hairpins, stem-loops) across Exons 10, 11, and 12, with user-tunable free energies ($\Delta G = -8$ to $-25\text{ kcal/mol}$).
2. **Ribosome Translocation & Pause Kinetics**: Simulates translating ribosomes slowing down at stable stem-loops, showing how local secondary structure modulates codon decoding rates and co-translational folding of CFTR NBD1.
3. **Antisense Oligonucleotide (ASO) & Small Molecule Unfolding**: Demonstrates the impact of steric-blocking ASOs or therapeutic interventions designed to unwind restrictive hairpins and alleviate translation barriers.
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Full WCAG 2.1 AAA accessible controls, slider inputs with live ARIA readout, colorblind-safe SVG/Canvas renderers, and zero external build dependencies.
