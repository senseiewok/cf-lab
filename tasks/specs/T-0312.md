## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical ciliary metachronal wave visualizer on `src/cf/tree/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/tree/index.html`:
1. **Kuramoto Hydrodynamic Synchronization**: Implements the coupled oscillator model $\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_{j=1}^N \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i - \alpha)$ simulating cooperative phase-locking of adjacent ciliary axonemes ($10-15\,\text{Hz}$) across bronchial epithelial surfaces.
2. **Viscoelastic Load Dampening**: Models the transition from coordinated hydrodynamic metachrony in healthy PCL viscosity ($\sim 1-2\,\text{mPa}\cdot\text{s}$) to desynchronized, stagnant ciliary entanglement under hyper-viscous CF mucus ($\sim 10^3-10^5\,\text{mPa}\cdot\text{s}$).
3. **Interactive Controls & Metrics**: Sliders for hydrodynamic coupling strength ($K$), mucus viscosity ($\eta$), ATP/purinergic stimulation ($\omega_0$), and ciliary density ($0.5-8\,\text{cilia}/\mu\text{m}^2$).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice medical boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_ciliary_metachronal_wave_explorer.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
