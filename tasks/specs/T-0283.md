## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#tight-junction-permeability-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate paracellular macromolecule transit and apical-to-basolateral tight junction remodeling in CF bronchial epithelium. Implements a biophysical pore-pathway model evaluating transepithelial electrical resistance (TEER $500\text{--}2500\ \Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$), transient tight junction modulation (via synthetic Claudin-1 peptidomimetics or EDTA chelation), and paracellular Stokes-Einstein diffusion kinetics for non-viral minicircle DNA and LNPs ($r_{\text{pore}} = 0.5\text{--}8.0\text{ nm}$). Provides dose-dependent permeation kinetics to target basal stem cells while preventing irreversible barrier breakdown or systemic macromolecule leak. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
