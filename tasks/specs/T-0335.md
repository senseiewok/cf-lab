## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical apical microvillar brush and glycocalyx diffusion simulator on `src/cf/delivery/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/delivery/index.html`:
1. **Apical Dense Brush Physics**: Simulates the dense microvillar brush border of human airway ciliated and secretory epithelial cells (packing density ~100-300 microvilli/$\mu\text{m}^2$, inter-villar spacing ~20-50 nm).
2. **Tethered MUC1/MUC4 Glycocalyx Barrier**: Models macromolecular steric hindrance and electrostatic exclusion for inhaled therapeutic nanoparticles (LNPs, viral capsids, ASOs) traversing the membrane-bound mucin brush to reach apical receptors or the plasma membrane.
3. **Diffusion Coefficient Scaling**: Calculates effective diffusion ($D_{\text{eff}} / D_0 = \exp(-\pi (r_p + r_f)^2 \cdot C_f)$) as a function of particle hydrodynamic radius ($r_p \approx 10-100\ \text{nm}$) and glycocalyx mesh fiber density.
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_microvillar_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
