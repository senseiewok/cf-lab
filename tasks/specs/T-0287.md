## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#pdi-redox-explorer` on `/cf/cftr/` to simulate redox-dependent allosteric remodeling of cryptic transmembrane cysteine residues (Cys-524 in TM10 and Cys-1395 in NBD2) by Protein Disulfide Isomerase (PDI) and endogenous S-nitrosoglutathione (GSNO). Simulates thiol-disulfide exchange kinetics, conformational stability shifts ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{redox}}$), open probability modulation across Class II (F508del) and Class III alleles, and therapeutic synergy with CFTR potentiators. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity and accessible SVG reaction coordinate visualization.
