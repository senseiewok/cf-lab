## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/vx522_inhaled_lnp_mucus_diffusion.py` modeling inhaled CFTR mRNA aerosol lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery through cystic fibrosis airway mucus:
1. **Mucus Mesh Steric Obstruction**: Formulates Ogston sieving and Renkin hydrodynamic hindrance scaling relating LNP diameter ($d_{\text{lnp}} \approx 80\ \text{nm}$) to heterogeneous mucin mesh pore diameter ($d_{\text{pore}} \in [100, 200]\ \text{nm}$).
2. **Surface Charge Neutralization**: Simulates DLVO double-layer electrostatic trap energy $|U_{\text{elec}}| < 1.0\ k_B T$ under near-neutral/zwitterionic surface charge ($\zeta \in [-2.0, +2.0]\ \text{mV}$).
3. **PEG-Lipid Shedding Kinetics**: Models shedding of hydrophilic PEG-lipids ($k_{\text{shed}} \in [0.05, 0.20]\ \text{h}^{-1}$) to permit apolipoprotein adsorption and endocytic uptake following mucus transit.
4. **Ionizable Lipid pKa Tuning**: Models pH-dependent protonation ($\alpha_{\text{prot}}$) switching from $< 10\%$ at extracellular pH 7.2 to $> 85\%$ at endosomal pH 5.5 for $\text{p}K_a \in [6.2, 6.5]$ to drive endosomal escape.
