## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops a biophysical transport model in `tools/discovery/ali_permeability_flux_model.py` evaluating drug permeation kinetics across mature airway epithelial barriers:
1. **Calu-3 ALI Transport Kinetics**: Calculates apparent permeability coefficients ($P_{\text{app}}$ in $\text{cm/s}$) and efflux ratios ($ER = P_{\text{app, B-to-A}} / P_{\text{app, A-to-B}}$) for small molecule modulators, antibiotics (tobramycin, colistin), and synthetic oligonucleotides.
2. **TEER & Paracellular Barrier Correlation**: Models the quantitative relationship between transepithelial electrical resistance ($\text{TEER} = 400-1200\ \Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$) and paracellular diffusion rate constant ($k_{\text{para}}$), capturing barrier loosening under mucolytic or permeation enhancer treatment.
3. **Efflux Transporter Phenotyping**: Accounts for apical P-glycoprotein (P-gp/ABCB1) and multidrug resistance protein (MRP1/ABCC1) active extrusion kinetics using Michaelis-Menten transport dynamics.
4. **Deterministic Verification**: Comprehensive unit test suite in `tools/discovery/test_ali_permeability_flux_model.py` with 100% parameter bounds validation.
