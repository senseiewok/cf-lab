## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/cftr/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling non-canonical nucleic acid secondary structures (G-quadruplexes / G4) in the human CFTR gene promoter and 5' untranslated region (5' UTR):
1. **G-Quadruplex Folding Thermodynamics**: Quantifies parallel vs antiparallel G-quartet stacking equilibria governed by monovalent cation coordination (K+ vs Na+) and temperature ($T_m$).
2. **Transcriptional & Translational Regulation**: Simulates how stabilizing promoter G-quadruplexes modulates RNA polymerase II elongation, while 5' UTR G-quadruplexes influence 43S pre-initiation complex ribosomal scanning and CFTR translation efficiency.
3. **Small-Molecule Ligand Stabilization**: Models binding thermodynamics ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{bind}}$) of G4-selective ligands (e.g. Pyridostatin, TMPyP4) and downstream expression shifts.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
