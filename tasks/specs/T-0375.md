## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/asl_electrostatic_binding_model.py` to model the electrostatic interaction kinetics and charge-neutralization binding between cationic genetic vectors (liposomes, LNPs, PEI, polymeric carriers) and polyanionic mucin sialic acid / sulfated glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) in CF airway surface liquid:
1. **Electrostatic Binding Equilibrium**: Models Debye-Hückel charge screening, zeta potential shifts, and ionic strength dependencies across human CF ASL ionic concentrations (NaCl 80–140 mM).
2. **Nanoparticle Immobilization & Aggregation**: Quantifies the threshold charge density (zeta potential > +15 mV) where electrostatic bridging causes catastrophic vector immobilization within the dense MUC5AC/MUC5B network.
3. **PEG / Polyampholyte Shielding Efficacy**: Evaluates dense hydrophilic PEGylation and zwitterionic coatings to prevent charge-mediated airway clearance and prolong mucosal residence time.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_asl_electrostatic_binding_model.py` and registers check rows in `check-all.ps1`.
