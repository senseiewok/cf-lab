## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive biophysical multi-cilia simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/delivery/` (with complete Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/delivery/`) modeling airway epithelial clearance:
1. **Ciliary Beat Frequency (CBF) Dynamics**: Models active ciliary beating across physiological frequencies (8 to 16 Hz) with asymmetry between power stroke and recovery stroke.
2. **Metachronal Wave Propagation**: Visualizes phase-lag synchronization across adjacent ciliated cells, demonstrating how coordinated metachronal waves drive directional mucus transport ($v = 40$ to $100\text{ }\mu\text{m/s}$).
3. **Mucus Viscous Load Damping**: Simulates the effect of dehydrated, hyper-concentrated CF mucus ($>8\%\text{ solids}$) on ciliary amplitude, stroke velocity, and wave coherence vs hydrated healthy ASL ($1.5\%\text{ solids}$).
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Full WCAG 2.1 AAA accessible controls, slider inputs with live ARIA readout, colorblind-safe SVG/Canvas renderers, and zero external build dependencies.
