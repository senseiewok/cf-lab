## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive, accessible biophysical simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/delivery/` (with complete Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/delivery/`) demonstrating apical glycocalyx barrier exclusion and enzymatic permeabilization:
1. **Periciliary Liquid & Glycocalyx Mesh Dynamics**: Models the dense apical glycocalyx layer (MUC1, MUC4, MUC16 tethered mucins, heparan sulfate, and sialic acid residues) on ciliated airway epithelial cells, evaluating steric mesh pore sizes ($15\text{ to }40\text{ nm}$).
2. **Nanoparticle Steric & Electrostatic Hindrance**: Simulates Brownian diffusion of LNPs, EVs, and AAV capsids through the charged glycocalyx barrier, illustrating why unmodified cationic or large particles undergo severe apical exclusion ($< 2\%$ apical-to-membrane flux).
3. **Exogenous Sialidase & Mucolytic Conditioning**: Demonstrates transient barrier opening via recombinant sialidase pretreatment (cleaving $\alpha 2,3$- and $\alpha 2,6$-linked sialic acids) or neutral PEGylation, restoring macromolecular access to the apical plasma membrane.
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Full WCAG 2.1 AAA accessible controls, slider inputs with live ARIA readout, colorblind-safe SVG/Canvas renderers, and zero external build dependencies.
