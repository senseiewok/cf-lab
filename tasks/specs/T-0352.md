## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/u1_snrnp_decoy_thermodynamics.py` in `cf-research` to quantify binding free energies, spliceosome competition, and pseudoexon exclusion dynamics for CFTR deep intronic splicing mutations:
1. **Splice Site Base Pairing Thermodynamics**: Implements SantaLucia nearest-neighbor hybridization models to compute duplex free energies ($\Delta G^\circ_{37}$) between 5' splice site cryptic motifs (e.g. 3849+10kbC->T: 5'-GUAUGU-3') and the 9-nt 5' end of endogenous U1 snRNA (5'-ACUUACCUG-3').
2. **Decoy Oligonucleotide / Engineered U1 Competition**: Computes competitive equilibrium constants ($K_d$) and Hill cooperativity for steric-blocking ASOs, phosphorodiamidate morpholino oligomers (PMOs), and engineered U1 snRNAs that target cryptic splice sites.
3. **Pseudoexon Exclusion Dose-Response Curve**: Generates dose-response curves predicting percentage exclusion of pseudoexons ($E_{\text{excl}} = 100 \cdot [D] / ([D] + IC_{50})$) and functional CFTR mRNA restoration in homozygous vs compound heterozygous models.
4. **Deterministic Validation**: Validates predictions against published minigene splicing assays and patient nasal epithelial cell RT-PCR measurements, providing `--check` self-tests.
