# T-0265: Open-Source Protein & Genomic Science Model Benchmark & Zero-Shot CFTR Mutational Landscape Evaluator

## Scientific & Curative Objective
Evaluate and integrate open-source biological foundation models (specifically Meta **ESM-2** `esm2_t33_650M_UR50D` / `esm2_t12_35M_UR50D` protein masked-marginal log-likelihoods, **ThermoMPNN / ProteinMPNN** structure-conditioned $\Delta\Delta G^{\circ}$ stability scoring on `PDB:5UAK`/`6MSM`/`8EIQ`, and **Evo-2 / Nucleotide Transformer** zero-shot intronic splice/regulatory likelihoods) against the lab's mechanistic biophysical models in `cf-research/tools/discovery/`.

Because open-source science models can run locally on CPU/GPU or via deterministic precomputed embeddings without sending patient data to external endpoints, this module (`tools/discovery/science_model_benchmark.py`) provides:
1. **Zero-shot missense & deamination bystander scoring ($\Delta\log P$)**: Cross-checks ESM-2 masked-marginal log-likelihood ratios and structure-conditioned $\Delta\Delta G^{\circ}$ predictions against `nbd1_thermodynamics.py` (`T-0199`) and `ptc_cryoem_allostery.py` (`T-0217`) across canonical CFTR class II/III/IV alleles (`F508del`, `G551D`, `N1303K`, `R117H`, `A455E`) and suppressor-tRNA/base-editing readthrough substitutions (`G542W/Q/R`, `W1282R/Q/L`).
2. **Calibration & concordance verifier**: Computes Spearman rank correlation ($\rho$), ROC-AUC for pathogenic vs benign *CFTR2* alleles, and false-discovery bounds when open-source foundation model scores are combined with our mechanistic MWC gating and ERAD thermodynamic models.
3. **Curative Discovery Council integration**: Registers `science_model_benchmark` in `DISCOVERY_MODULE_REGISTRY` (`tools/discovery/discovery_council.py`) with offline deterministic fixtures so CI runs in `<1s` without requiring multi-gigabyte model weight downloads.
