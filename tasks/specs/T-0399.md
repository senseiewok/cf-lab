## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling dual adeno-associated viral (AAV) vector payload partitioning and protein trans-splicing reconstitution for full-length CFTR:
1. **Payload Size-Limit Packaging**: Resolves the canonical 4.7 kb single-AAV packaging constraint by partitioning the 4.44 kb CFTR coding sequence into N-terminal (exons 1-13) and C-terminal (exons 14-27) split fragments.
2. **Split-Intein Trans-Splicing Kinetics**: Models the rapid, traceless catalytic ligation mediated by Nostoc punctiforme (*Npu*) DnaE split-intein ($k_{\text{splice}} \approx 1.1 \times 10^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$), yielding full-length functional CFTR channel.
3. **Co-Transduction Poisson Statistics**: Simulates dose-dependent multi-vector entry per airway epithelial cell to quantify fractional full-length reconstitution vs orphan half-channels.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
