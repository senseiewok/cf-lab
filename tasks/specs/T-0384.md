## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/cftr/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling the free-energy landscape and catalytic ATP hydrolysis cycle of CFTR Nucleotide-Binding Domain 2 (NBD2):
1. **Catalytic Site 2 Gating Free-Energy Surface**: Visualizes the thermodynamic landscape spanning ATP binding, NBD1-NBD2 tight dimer formation (open pore conduction state), catalytic transition state barrier ($\Delta G^\ddagger \approx 18.5\text{ kcal/mol}$), and post-hydrolysis dimer dissociation into the closed state.
2. **Potentiator Gating Kinetic Modulation**: Explores how CFTR potentiators (ivacaftor, deutivacaftor) stabilize the open burst conformation without abolishing basal ATP turnover, prolonging burst duration ($T_o$).
3. **Interactive Reaction Coordinate**: Interactive 2D free-energy reaction coordinate slider allowing researchers to modulate temperature, ATP concentration, and catalytic water coordination barrier ($\Delta G_{\text{cat}}$).
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
