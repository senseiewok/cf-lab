## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/ptc_readthrough_cooperativity_model.py` to quantify translation elongation kinetics, amino acid misincorporation fidelity, and full-length functional CFTR yield under synergistic readthrough combinations:
1. **Ribosome Decoding Site Kinetic Model**: Formulates the four-state Markov model of ribosome stop codon decoding: elongation stall, eRF1 binding, near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA accommodation, and peptide chain termination vs. readthrough.
2. **Dual Pharmacophore Synergy Formulation**: Quantifies the cooperativity coefficient $\alpha$ (Bliss independence vs. Loewe additivity) of synthetic aminoglycosides (ELX-02 / NB124) combined with release factor inhibitors / eRF1 degraders (CC-90009 / SRI-37240).
3. **Variant-Specific Sequence Context Weighting**: Evaluates readthrough efficiency across stop codon identities ($\text{UGA} > \text{UAG} \gg \text{UAA}$) and $+4$ nucleotide context ($\text{C} > \text{U} > \text{A} \ge \text{G}$) for G542X, W1282X, R553X, R1162X, and Y122X.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_ptc_readthrough_cooperativity_model.py` and registers check rows in `cf-research` test runners.
