## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/metachronal_wave_damping_model.py` in `cf-research` to model ciliary hydrodynamics and mucus clearance under normal vs cystic fibrosis conditions:
1. **Hydrodynamic Metachronal Coupling**: Simulates hydrodynamic interactions between neighboring cilia rows using Stokes flow approximations, determining phase-locking stability and wavelength ($\lambda \approx 20\text{--}35\text{ }\mu\text{m}$).
2. **Viscoelastic Load Damping**: Models the Maxwell viscoelastic response of airway mucus (elastic modulus $G'$, loss modulus $G''$), calculating amplitude damping and frequency attenuation as solids content increases from $1.5\%$ to $>8\%$.
3. **Transport Velocity & Shear Stress Outputs**: Computes net mucociliary transport velocity ($v_{\text{transport}}$) and epithelial shear stress across varying periciliary layer (PCL) depths ($7\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ healthy vs collapsed $<3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ CF).
4. **Deterministic Validation**: Validates model against published micro-rheology and bead-tracking clearance measurements and registers self-tests.
