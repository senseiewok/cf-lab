## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical ASL pH regulation simulator on `src/cf/tree/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/tree/index.html`:
1. **ASL Acidification Biophysics**: Models steady-state ASL pH as a balance between CFTR bicarbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$) secretion, apical ATP12A ($\text{H}^+/\text{K}^+$-ATPase) / vacuolar $\text{H}^+$-ATPase proton pumping, and paracellular carbonic anhydrase buffering ($\text{pH} \approx 6.8$ in CF vs. $7.4$ in healthy airway).
2. **Antimicrobial Peptide (AMP) Activity Curve**: Visualizes the steep sigmoidal inactivation of endogenous host defense peptides (hBD-1, lactoferrin, lysozyme) in acidic ASL and rescue under potassium-competitive ATP12A inhibitor (ouabain / vonoprazan analogs) or bicarbonate potentiators.
3. **Interactive Controls & Diagnostics**: Sliders for CFTR $\text{HCO}_3^-$ permeability ratio ($P_{\text{HCO}_3}/P_{\text{Cl}} = 0.2-0.4$), ATP12A expression density, and aerosolized buffer delivery (tromethamine / sodium bicarbonate inhalation).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice medical boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_asl_ph_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
