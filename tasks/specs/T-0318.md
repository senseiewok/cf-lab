## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive nonsense readthrough pharmacodynamics visualizer on `src/cf/ptc/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/ptc/index.html`:
1. **Ribosomal Decoding Center Kinetics**: Models the kinetic competition between near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA misincorporation and eukaryotic release factors (eRF1-eRF3-GTP) at premature termination codons (UGA, UAG, UAA).
2. **Dual-Agent Synergistic Potentiation**: Simulates readthrough efficiency gains of combining synthetic aminoglycosides (e.g. ELX-02 / NB124) with eRF1/eRF3-targeting degraders (CC-90009 / SRI-37240 / SRI-41315), demonstrating super-additive full-length CFTR protein restoration (>5-10% of wild-type).
3. **Allele-Specific Readthrough Matrix**: Parameterized across canonical Class I alleles with distinct stop contexts: G542X (UGA + C), W1282X (UGA + A), R553X (UGA + A), R1162X (UGA + G), and Y122X (UAA).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice medical boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_ptc_readthrough_synergy_explorer.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
