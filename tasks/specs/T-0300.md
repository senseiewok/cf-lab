## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Mounts `#glycocalyx-permeabilization-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to showcase enzymatic remodeling of the dense apical ciliated cell glycocalyx barrier for enhanced genetic vector delivery grounded in `tools/discovery/glycocalyx_permeabilization_model.py` (T-0291):
1. **Ogston Steric Sieving & Dense Mucin Polymer Brush**: Models steric exclusion of inhaled LNPs ($d \sim 80\text{--}120\text{ nm}$) and AAVs ($d \sim 25\text{ nm}$) through apical membrane-tethered mucin brushes (MUC1, MUC4, MUC16).
2. **Enzymatic Desialylation Kinetics**: Simulates neuraminidase/sialidase cleavage of terminal $\alpha\text{-}(2\to3)$ and $\alpha\text{-}(2\to6)$ sialic acid residues, reducing negative surface charge density and steric polymer brush height ($H_0 \sim 250\text{ nm} \to H_f \sim 60\text{ nm}$).
3. **Macromolecular Vector Flux Acceleration**: Computes effective pore size distribution and vector permeation flux to the underlying apical plasma membrane and basal stem cells.
4. **Mexican Spanish Parity (`es-MX`)**: Full bilingual UI integration with natural Mexican Spanish terminology (`equipo médico`, `computadora`) and verified medical boundary disclaimers.
5. **Strict Zero-Build & CSP Hygiene**: Built entirely with native ES modules, accessible responsive SVG graphics, and zero unsafe DOM injection.
