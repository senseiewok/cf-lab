## 1. Curative Mission & User Value

Translates the new *CFTR* RefSeq (`NM_000492.4`) sequence-window scanner (`T-0264`, `cftr_sequence_scanner.py`) and zero-shot biological foundation model benchmark (`T-0265`, `science_model_benchmark.py`) into a bilingual (English + Spanish) interactive research card and explorer on `senseiewok.ai`. Allows patients, families, and researchers to inspect how **SpCas9 / SpG / SpRY** base-editing windows, **intronic cryptic splice ASOs** (`3849+10kbC>T`), **ESM-2** evolutionary log-likelihoods, and **ProteinMPNN / ThermoMPNN** structural $\Delta\Delta G^{\circ}$ (on `PDB:5UAK` / `6MSM` / `8EIQ`) complement each other across mutation classes.
