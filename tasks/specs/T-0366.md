## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling inhaled superparamagnetic iron oxide nanoparticle (SPION) aerosol transport, external magnetic gradient field focusing, and theranostic magnetic resonance (MRI) T2* contrast enhancement in Cystic Fibrosis airways:
1. **Magnetic vs Viscous Drag Hydrodynamics**: Models the force equilibrium between Stokes hydrodynamic drag within viscoelastic CF airway mucus (shear-thinning Carreau-Yasuda model) and external magnetic force (F_mag = (m · grad) B) applied via external permanent magnets or Halbach arrays.
2. **Overcoming Mucociliary Clearance Deadlines**: Quantifies the required magnetic field gradient (T/m) to accelerate nanoparticle trans-mucus transit time below the baseline mucociliary clearance cutoff (10-30 minutes), preventing premature clearance into the esophagus.
3. **Core-Shell Biodegradable Architectures**: Explores PEGylated vs dextran-coated SPION delivery vehicles carrying gene-editing payloads (LNP-mRNA complexes or Cas9 ribonucleoproteins) and visualizes regional deposition in peripheral obstructed bronchioles.
4. **Accessible, Zero-Build Architecture**: Implements native ES modules with accessible magnetic field vector visualization, interactive parameter tuning, ARIA landmarks, and strict dignity language boundaries.
