# T-0223: Interactive Clinical Trial Eligibility, Washout & Endpoint Plain-Language Decoder

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track B)
Navigating interventional Cystic Fibrosis clinical trials is notoriously daunting for patients and caregivers due to technical jargon: "4-week modulator washout", "ppFEV1 between 40% and 90%", "concomitant CYP3A4 inhibitors", and "absolute change in sweat chloride". For individuals with rare nonsense or canonical splice variants, finding active trials evaluating non-modulator therapies (mRNA, ASO, ENaC inhibitors, genetic medicines) requires clarity on trial requirements.

This specification directs the development of an accessible, interactive Clinical Trial Decoder on `senseiewok.ai` under `/cf/trials/` that translates clinical eligibility criteria, required washout periods, and primary endpoints into warm, dignified, plain-language patient guides with Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) parity.

## 2. Primary Evidence Grounding
- **Inhaled Genetic Therapies**: Study of mRNA LNP inhalation in patients with CF homozygous or heterozygous for non-responsive CFTR mutations. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT05248230`.
- **Inhaled ENaC Channel Inhibitors**: Phase 1/2 study of novel inhaled ENaC blocker for CF lung hydration. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT05668741`.
- **First-in-Class NBD1 Correctors**: Phase 1 clinical evaluation of small molecule stabilizing CFTR NBD1 unfolding. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT06429176`.
- **Catalog Dataset**: `cf-research/tools/trial_atlas/snapshot.json` containing verified ClinicalTrials.gov protocol records.

## 3. Architecture & User Interface Design
- **DOM Structure & Interactive Elements**:
  - Container: `<section id="trials-decoder-section" class="tool-section" aria-labelledby="trials-decoder-heading">`
  - Filter Bar: `<div id="trial-filters" class="filter-controls">`
    - Modality filter: All, Genetic/mRNA, Small Molecule Corrector/Potentiator, Alternative Targets (ENaC, TMEM16A), Anti-infective/Phage.
    - Genotype selector: Class I (Nonsense/Splice), Class II (F508del), Rare/Uncharacterized.
  - Interactive Washout Timeline: `<svg id="trial-washout-timeline-svg" viewBox="0 0 760 180" role="img" aria-label="Visual timeline of medication washout and screening visits">`
    - Shows baseline status, washout window (days/weeks), screening visit, first dose, and endpoint assessment points.
    - Motion toggle: `<button type="button" class="svg-motion-toggle" data-svg-target="trial-washout-timeline-svg" aria-pressed="false">Pause</button>`
  - Plain-Language Cards Grid: `<div id="trial-cards-grid" class="cards-grid">`
    - Each card displays NCT ID, plain title, phase, delivery route (Aerosol, Oral, IV), eligibility summary ("Who can join"), and primary outcome ("What doctors measure").
- **Design & Accessibility Guardrails**:
  - CSS Grid with `min-width: 0` on children to eliminate mobile horizontal scrolling at 375px.
  - Spanish localization in `src/locales/es-MX.json` ensuring empathetic, medically rigorous Spanish terminology (avoiding robotic automated translation).

## 4. Deliverables & Target Files
- In repository `.ai`:
  - `src/cf/trials/index.html`: Layout, semantic HTML5 sections, filter landmarks, and accessible schema tags.
  - `src/scripts/trials-explorer.js`: ES module handling trial filtering, interactive card rendering, timeline visualization, and modal plain-language explanations.
  - `src/locales/es-MX.json`: Spanish translations for all trial decoder components.
  - `tests/test_trials_page.py`: Unit tests verifying rendering of trial cards, filter operations, and zero console errors.
