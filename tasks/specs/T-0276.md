## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#mrna-velocity-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate CFTR synthetic mRNA translation kinetics and ribosome elongation dynamics. Implements the Totally Asymmetric Simple Exclusion Process (TASEP) across 1,480 codons, modeling codon-specific elongation rates, ribosome crowding, and collision-induced No-Go Decay (NGD) risk. Evaluates 3' UTR polyadenylation tail decay ($k_{\text{deaden}} = 0.08\text{ nt/min}$) comparing 100-120 nt optimized poly(A) tails against non-stabilized constructs, with $N^1$-methylpseudouridine ($\text{m}^1\Psi$) modification suppressing TLR7/8 innate immune activation. Computes full-length CFTR protein output (molecules/mRNA) and apical chloride conductance rescue vs 10% therapeutic threshold. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
