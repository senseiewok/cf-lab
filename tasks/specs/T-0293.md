## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#macrophage-evasion-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate pulmonary alveolar macrophage phagocytosis evasion kinetics for aerosolized genetic delivery vectors. Implements biophysical scavenger receptor recognition (SR-A1, CD36, MARCO), lipid-polymer hybrid nanoparticle (LPN) surface zwitterionic / CD47 "don't-eat-me" stealth peptide passivation, and residence time optimization in the airway lumen. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity and accessible SVG phagocytosis inhibition curves.
