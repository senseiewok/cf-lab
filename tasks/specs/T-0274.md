## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#intronic-splice-explorer` on `/cf/cftr/` to model steric-blocking antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) rescue across deep intronic and non-canonical splicing CFTR mutations (`3849+10kbC->T`, `2789+5G->A`, `1811+1.6kbA->G`, `3272-26A->G`). Couples SpliceAI donor/acceptor delta scores, Evo-2 genomic log-odds, and SantaLucia (1998) nearest-neighbor hybridization thermodynamics ($T_m$, $\Delta G^\circ$) with Hill cooperative concentration titration (0-500 nM), full-length mRNA restoration fraction ($f_{\text{WT}}$), and polarized apical chloride current restoration ($I_{\text{Cl}} = 100 \times f_{\text{WT}}^{1.15}$) compared against 10% therapeutic and 15% curative thresholds. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
