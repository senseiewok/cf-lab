## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical nonsense mutation readthrough synergy landscape on `src/cf/ptc/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/ptc/index.html`:
1. **Dual-Mechanism Readthrough Cooperativity**: Visualizes non-linear synergy (Loewe additivity and Bliss independence matrix) between decoding-center mispairing agents (ELX-02, G418) and eukaryotic release factor antagonists (SRI-37240, CC-90009 targeting eRF1/GSPT1).
2. **Nonsense Codon & Flanking Context Bias**: Models differential full-length CFTR restoration across nonsense alleles: UGA (+4 C, e.g. W1282X) yielding highest readthrough efficacy vs. UAA / UAG codons with refractory termination complexes.
3. **Interactive Potency & Toxicity Sliders**: Interactive response surface mapping readthrough percentage ($0.5-12\%$ of wild-type CFTR) against misincorporation toxicity thresholds and auditory hair cell ototoxicity boundaries.
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
