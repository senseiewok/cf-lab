## 1. Curative Mission & Scientific Scope

Models endoplasmic reticulum-associated degradation (ERAD) escape kinetics for misfolded CFTR (F508del, N1303K, G85E) regulated by protein disulfide isomerase (PDI / PDIA1) redox remodeling and S-nitrosoglutathione (GSNO) / S-nitrosylation of cryptic cysteine residues (Cys524, Cys1355). Establishes multi-state kinetic transition models quantifying PDI oxidoreductase activity vs calnexin/Hsp70-Hsp90 chaperone triage, estimating surface functional rescue fraction ($f_{\text{surf}}$) and sweat chloride response equivalents.
