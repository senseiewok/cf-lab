## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/glycocalyx_permeabilization_model.py` to simulate the dense periciliary glycocalyx meshwork (tethered mucins MUC1, MUC4, MUC16 and heparan sulfate proteoglycans, brush thickness $H \sim 200\text{--}500\text{ nm}$, steric mesh spacing $\xi \sim 20\text{--}40\text{ nm}$) on human CF airway epithelial ciliated cells. The model:
1. Simulates steric and charge-dependent exclusion of inhaled therapeutic nanoparticles (LNPs $d \sim 80\text{--}120\text{ nm}$ vs. AAV $d \sim 25\text{ nm}$) using Ogston sieving and de Gennes polymer brush scaling.
2. Models enzymatic cleavage kinetics of terminal sialic acid caps by exogenous sialidase/neuraminidase and glycosaminoglycan depolymerization by heparinase.
3. Computes time-dependent mesh dilation ($\xi(t)$), effective brush height collapse, and vector apical membrane collision frequency.
4. Registers the new module in `DISCOVERY_MODULE_REGISTRY` in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py`.
