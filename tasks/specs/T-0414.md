## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/basal_stem_quiescence_kinetics.py` modeling human airway basal progenitor cell (KRT5+/TP63+) turnover, quiescence exit, and long-term clonal persistence following inhaled gene addition or genome editing:
1. **Basal Progenitor Quiescence & Activation**: Formulates homeostatic stem cell division ($\lambda_{\text{div}} \approx 0.005\text{--}0.02\ \text{day}^{-1}$) vs injury-induced activation following viral transduction or epithelial injury.
2. **Transit-Amplifying Clonal Dynamics**: Models asymmetric vs symmetric division modes ($P_{\text{sym}} \in [0.1, 0.4]$) preserving progenitor pools while driving differentiation into ciliated and secretory daughter cells.
3. **Multi-Year Transgene Retention**: Derives longevity equations for permanent basal integration versus non-integrating episomal vector washout ($t_{1/2} \approx 90\text{--}120\ \text{days}$).
4. **Therapeutic Threshold Maintenance**: Predicts sustained transepithelial apical CFTR channel conductance ($\ge 10\%\ \text{WT}$) across decades of epithelial turnover.
