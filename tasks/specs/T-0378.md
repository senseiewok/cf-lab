## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling airway epithelial goblet cell hyperplasia, mucin polymeric crosslinking, and non-linear rheological yield stress under CF pathology:
1. **MUC5AC vs MUC5B Polymeric Stoichiometry**: Contrasts homeostatic baseline (MUC5B-predominant, porous network permitting mucociliary clearance) with chronic CF inflammation / IL-13-driven goblet cell metaplasia (MUC5AC-predominant, dense hyper-adhesive strands).
2. **Mucus Gelation & Ciliary Arrest**: Simulates how altered mucin concentration and calcium-dependent disulfide crosslinking cause dramatic non-Newtonian yield stress spikes ($\tau_y \propto [MUC5AC]^{2.3}$) that overwhelm ciliary propulsion and immobilize aerosolized nanomedicines.
3. **Interactive Mucolytic & Chelation Interventions**: Sliders for reducing agent concentration (NAC, dithiothreitol equivalents), calcium chelators (EDTA/citrate), and recombinant human DNase to quantify restoration of ciliary clearance and particle diffusivity.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
