## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling pulmonary alveolar macrophage clearance evasion kinetics and biomimetic brush shielding:
1. **Macrophage Phagocytosis Evasion Kinetics**: Simulates how synthetic nanocarriers (LNPs, polymeric nanoparticles, exosomes) evade Scavenger Receptor Class A (SR-A) and Toll-like receptor (TLR7/8) uptake by resident alveolar macrophages in inflamed CF airways.
2. **Biomimetic CD47 "Don't Eat Me" Signaling**: Models CD47-SIRPα binding affinity ($K_D \approx 1\ \mu\text{M}$) and inhibitory phosphatase recruitment (SHP-1/SHP-2) that deactivates macrophage non-muscle myosin IIA cytoskeleton activation.
3. **Zwitterionic & Stealth Brush Architecture**: Compares dense polysarcosine (pSar) and zwitterionic brush densities against traditional PEGylated shells, quantifying reduced protein corona formation and anti-PEG antibody neutralization evasion.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
