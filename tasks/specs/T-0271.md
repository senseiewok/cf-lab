## 1. Curative Mission & Discovery Science Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/macrophage_evasion_kinetics.py` to model the clearance of aerosolized lipid-polymer hybrid nanoparticles (LPNs) by human airway and alveolar macrophages. Simulates competitive scavenger receptor (SR-A1, CD36) binding, opsonin adsorption (surfactant protein A/D, complement C3b), and stealth surface functionalization (dense PEG brush, CD47 "don't eat me" biomimetic peptides). Computes macrophage uptake velocity ($V_{\text{max}}, K_m$), evasion half-life ($t_{1/2}$), and epithelial retention fraction ensuring inhaled genetic medicines reach basal epithelial stem cells before phagocytic clearance.
