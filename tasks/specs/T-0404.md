## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/delivery/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling inhaled mRNA-LNP aerosol delivery through thick cystic fibrosis mucus:
1. **Mucus Viscoelastic Steric Sieving**: Models hydrodynamic sieving across dense CF mucin polymer networks ($30\text{--}120\text{ nm}$ pore mesh) as a function of mucus solids percentage ($2.0\text{--}8.0\text{ wt}\%$) and shear modulus $G'(\omega)$.
2. **Nanoparticle Surface Charge Neutralization & Stealth Brush**: Evaluates zwitterionic and dense PEG brush architectures ($>5\text{ mol}\%$) shielding cationic core charges to minimize electrostatic entrapment by anionic mucin glycans.
3. **Ionizable Lipid pKa & Endosomal Escape**: Simulates pH-dependent amine protonation inside acidic endosomes ($\text{p}K_a \approx 6.2\text{--}6.6$) triggering inverted hexagonal phase transition and mRNA cytosolic release.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) dictionary parity in `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js`.
