## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a mechanistic biophysical model in `tools/discovery/nbd2_atp_hydrolysis_model.py` quantifying the catalytic hydrolysis and NBD1-NBD2 dimer dissociation cycle that dictates CFTR channel closing:
1. **Catalytic Site 2 Hydrolysis Kinetics**: Models composite ATP binding Site 2 (formed by NBD2 Walker A/B motifs and NBD1 ABC signature motif LSGGQ), catalytic transition state barrier ($\Delta G^\ddagger \approx 18.5\text{ kcal/mol}$), and gamma-phosphate release.
2. **Dimer Relaxation & Channel Deactivation**: Simulates post-hydrolytic conformational relaxation, free-energy loss at the NBD interface ($\Delta \Delta G_{\text{dimer}}$), and the dissociation rate constant $k_{\text{off}}$ terminating ion conduction bursts.
3. **Potentiator Allosteric Stabilization**: Maps ivacaftor and deuterated potentiator binding modes that prolong open burst duration ($T_o$) by slowing Site 2 post-hydrolytic decoupling without abolishing basal ATP turnover.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive test suite in `tools/discovery/test_nbd2_atp_hydrolysis_model.py`.
