## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/trials/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) transforming the raw 940 interventional trial dataset (`data/care_team_trials_directory.json` from ClinicalTrials.gov API v2) into an accessible clinical translation and visit-preparation interface:
1. **Interactive Recruiting Studies Directory**:
   - Live browser of 57 currently recruiting interventional CF clinical trials across all therapeutic modalities (CFTR Modulators, AAV / Gene Therapy, mRNA Replacement, Bacteriophage, ENaC Inhibitors, and Other).
   - Real-time search by study title, NCT ID, lead sponsor, and intervention names.
   - Filter dropdown by trial phase (Phase 1, 2, 3, 4, NA, Early Phase 1).
   - Live KPI readouts for recruiting trial counts, active interventional status, and verified registry snapshot date (`2026-10-14`).
   - Detailed trial cards with NCT official registry hyperlinks, status, phase, lead sponsor, named interventions, and registered primary outcome with time frame verbatim.
2. **Bilingual Clinic Visit-Prep Sheet**:
   - Interactive language toggle between English and Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`).
   - Urgent care alert box (*"Call urgent line or 911 immediately if symptoms are new or severe"*).
   - Three structured, open questions to ask accredited CF care teams.
   - What-to-bring checklist and strict privacy reminder (never write PHI).
   - One-click "Copy Printable Visit-Prep Sheet" with accessible clipboard feedback banner.
3. **Strict Zero-Build Modern Standards & Security**:
   - Zero inline styles, zero `innerHTML` or `outerHTML` string assignment sinks (strict CSP compliant DOM creation using `document.createElement`, `setAttribute`, and `textContent`).
   - Zero prohibited words (`cure`, `miracle`, `battle`, `guarantee`, `cura`, `milagro`, `batalla`, `garantía`).
   - Exact verbatim NLM disclaimer (*"ClinicalTrials.gov does not evaluate safety or efficacy..."*) and laboratory dignity boundary (*"The lab does not assess efficacy or recommend treatments..."*).
   - 100% exact key and value parity between `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js`.

## 2. Primary Source Grounding

- Source Snapshot: `data/care_team_trials_directory.json` (compiled by `tools/trial_atlas/care_team_trials.py` from 940 PRISMA-screened interventional CF trials in ClinicalTrials.gov API v2).
- Boundary Standards: `cf-visit-prep` + `cf-ai-safe-use` clinical boundary contracts.
- Verified Benchmark Studies: `NCT05248230` (4D-710 AAV), `NCT05668741` (VX-522 mRNA), `NCT06429176` (SPL84 ASO), `NCT06132048` (MuCopilot).
