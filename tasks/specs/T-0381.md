## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a mechanistic mathematical model in `tools/discovery/nanoparticle_partitioning_model.py` evaluating the kinetic partitioning of inhaled aerosolized nanoparticles (LNP, polymeric, lipid-polymer hybrid) between mucociliary advective transit and alveolar macrophage phagocytic uptake:
1. **Advective vs Phagocytic Flux Competition**: Formulates ordinary differential equations coupling airway surface liquid advective transport ($v_{\text{mcc}} = 1\text{--}5\text{ mm/min}$) with size-dependent macrophage engulfment kinetics ($k_{\text{phag}} = k_0 \cdot \frac{d_{\text{np}}}{d_{\text{np}} + K_m}$).
2. **Surface Chemistry & PEGylation Escape Dynamics**: Incorporates particle surface charge (zeta potential $\zeta$) and PEG corona density ($\Gamma_{\text{PEG}}$) into phagocytic recognition avoidance factors and mucus steric mesh obstruction coefficients.
3. **Epithelial Transcytosis Window**: Calculates the fractional dose $f_{\text{epi}}$ achieving successful basal membrane translocation prior to clearance, generating actionable design criteria for CF gene delivery vectors.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive test suite in `tools/discovery/test_nanoparticle_partitioning_model.py`.
