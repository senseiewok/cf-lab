## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive browser simulation widget on `senseiewok.ai/cf/delivery/` translating the biophysical discoveries of `T-0393` (split-intein dual-AAV reconstitution and vector redosing kinetics) to public understanding:
1. **Interactive Vector Stoichiometry Controls**: Sliders allowing users to modulate Vector N and Vector C MOI ($0.5\text{--}10.0$), observing the live Poisson distribution of single- vs dual-positive transduced airway epithelial cells.
2. **Intein Kinetic Comparison**: Toggle between engineered split-inteins (Cfa, Npu DnaE, gp41-1), visualizing association free energy ($\Delta G^\circ$), trans-splicing half-life ($t_{1/2}$), and steady-state 170 kDa mature CFTR Band C yield.
3. **Multi-Dose Neutralizing Antibody Trajectory**: Dynamic multi-dose chart simulating repeat aerosol administrations across configurable intervals ($30\text{--}180\ \text{days}$), demonstrating neutralizing antibody titer accumulation and chloride transport attenuation.
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Spanish (`es-MX`) translation on `/es/delivery/` with exact clinical boundaries, zero false promises, and mandatory NLM ClinicalTrials.gov attribution.
