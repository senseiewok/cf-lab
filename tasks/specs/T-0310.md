## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements a quantitative codon-resolution ribosome elongation and termination kinetic model in `tools/discovery/ptc_ribosome_profiling_model.py`:
1. **Ribosomal Stall & Readthrough Rates**: Models termination competition kinetics at premature termination codons between eukaryotic release factors (eRF1/eRF3-GTP complex) and near-cognate tRNAs ($k_{\text{term}} \gg k_{\text{read}}$).
2. **Allele-Specific Readthrough Profiling**: Compares suppression efficiencies and readthrough amino acid incorporation frequencies across common CFTR nonsense mutations:
   - W1282X (UGA + C context, highest basal and induced readthrough)
   - G542X (UGA + A context)
   - R553X (UGA + G context)
   - R1162X (UGA + U context)
3. **Compound Synergy**: Models dose-dependent potentiation under co-treatment with aminoglycosides/ELX-02 and nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) inhibitors (SMG1i).
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit test suite in `tools/discovery/test_ptc_ribosome_profiling_model.py` and anti-hallucination validation.
