## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/tree/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling mutation-agnostic bypass of defective CFTR via calcium-activated chloride channel TMEM16A (Anoctamin-1 / ANO1):
1. **Calcium-Activation Thermodynamics**: Simulates Hill cooperativity of dual $Ca^{2+}$-binding sites (cooperativity coefficient $n_H \approx 2.1$, $EC_{50} \approx 420\text{ nM}$) and voltage-dependent gating transitions.
2. **Alternative Epithelial Fluid Secretion**: Computes apical chloride efflux ($J_{Cl}$) and airway surface liquid (ASL) height restoration compensating for complete CFTR loss across severe Class I/II genotypes.
3. **P2Y2 Receptor & IP3 Signaling**: Models purinergic receptor agonism (inhaled ATP/UTP/denufosol analogs) inducing transient vs sustained intracellular $Ca^{2+}$ elevation.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
