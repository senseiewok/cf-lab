## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#aerosol-hygroscopic-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to model inhaled micro-aerosol droplet evaporation and hygroscopic equilibrium dynamics across conducting airways. Simulates Mason equation heat- and mass-transfer coupling in humidified airways (relative humidity 90-99.5%, $T = 37^\circ\text{C}$), Köhler thermodynamic equilibrium for saline and excipient osmolyte solutions, and shifts in aerodynamic mass median diameter (MMAD $1.0\text{--}5.0\ \mu\text{m}$) dictating tracheobronchial vs alveolar deposition fractions. Compares vibrating mesh nebulizers against dry powder inhalers (DPI) to identify aerosol droplet stability envelopes for genetic payloads (mRNA-LNPs, AAVs). Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
