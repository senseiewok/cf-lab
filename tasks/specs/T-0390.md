## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/cftr/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling allosteric cooperativity and thermodynamic free-energy coupling ($\Delta\Delta G$) of small-molecule potentiators across CFTR gating mutants:
1. **Allosteric Cooperativity & Free-Energy Coupling**: Simulates how ivacaftor, deutivacaftor, and vanzacaftor stabilize the open-pore conformation via transmembrane helices TM4/TM5/TM8 cavity docking, coupling NBD dimerization transitions to pore opening probability ($P_o$).
2. **Genotype & Mutant Heterogeneity**: Explores differential allosteric energy coupling across gating class III archetypes (G551D, G1244E, G178R) versus misfolding class II mutations (F508del under correctors).
3. **Interactive Potentiator Titration**: Controls for drug concentration, ATP hydrolysis cycle states, and allosteric cooperativity factor ($\alpha$), displaying live Hill-equation shifts and thermodynamic state equilibria.
4. **Complete Localization**: Full parity in Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) via `data-i18n` attributes and verified against `tasks/check_i18n_parity.py`.
