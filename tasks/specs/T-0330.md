## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical ribosome translation termination vs. readthrough simulator on `src/cf/ptc/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/ptc/index.html`:
1. **Competition Kinetics**: Models kinetic competition between eukaryotic release factor ternary complex (eRF1-eRF3-GTP) and near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNA (aa-tRNA) binding at CFTR PTC sites (G542X/UGA, W1282X/UGA, R553X/UGA, Y122X/UAA).
2. **Stop Codon Identity & Basal Readthrough**: Renders differential baseline readthrough hierarchy ($UGA > UAG > UAA$) and +4 flanking nucleotide context ($C > U > A > G$).
3. **Pharmacological Readthrough Modulators**: Simulates concentration-dependent readthrough stimulation by small-molecule agents (ELX-02, G418, ataluren, CC-90009) and synergistic combination with NMD inhibitors (NMDI-14, SMG1i).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_ptc_ribosome_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
