## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/goblet_cell_mucus_stoichiometry.py` to quantitatively model IL-13/STAT6-driven goblet cell metaplasia, pathological MUC5AC hypersecretion, and protective MUC5B stoichiometric ratios in CF airway epithelia:
1. **Goblet Cell Plasticity & Differentiation**: Models basal-to-secretory-to-goblet cell transdifferentiation kinetics driven by chronic inflammation (IL-13, IL-1beta, TNF-alpha) and Notch/SPDEF signaling networks.
2. **MUC5AC vs MUC5B Secretion Stoichiometry**: Quantifies the shift from healthy airway mucus (dominated by MUC5B bundles facilitating mucociliary transport) to pathological CF mucus (excessive MUC5AC hypersecretion tethering to epithelial surfaces and forming stubborn adhesive plugs).
3. **Viscoelastic Plug Formation Thresholds**: Computes the critical MUC5AC:MUC5B ratio (>1.5) that triggers mucociliary transport arrest and static obstruction.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests, JSON export, and schema verification in `tools/discovery/test_goblet_cell_mucus_stoichiometry.py`.
