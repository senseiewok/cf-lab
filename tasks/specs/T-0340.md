## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/ribosome_profiling_decoding_model.py` to simulate ribo-seq ribosome stall dynamics and translational elongation velocities across CFTR premature termination codons (PTC):
1. **Ribosome Stall Kinetics at PTC Sites**: Quantifies translation arrest at UGA (G542X, R553X) and UGA (W1282X) stop codons versus surrounding codon contexts (-3 to +6 nucleotides).
2. **Near-Cognate tRNA Competition**: Models competition between near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs (Trp, Arg, Cys for UGA) and release factors (eRF1-eRF3) for the ribosomal A-site during basal and drug-stimulated readthrough (ELX-02, aminoglycosides).
3. **Collateral Ribosome Collisions**: Simulates disome/trisome formation downstream of slow decoding codons and activation of the Ribosome-associated Quality Control (RQC) pathway.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_ribosome_profiling_decoding_model.py`.
