# T-0233: Nonviral Minimal DNA Vector (minidnacftr) CpG Depletion & TLR9 Evasion Simulation

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track A)
A major bottleneck in nonviral gene addition and suppressor tRNA delivery to the human airway is toll-like receptor 9 (TLR9) activation. Unmethylated bacterial CpG dinucleotides present in standard plasmid backbones trigger robust endosomal TLR9 signaling in airway macrophages and dendritic cells, causing severe pro-inflammatory cytokine secretion (IFN-beta, IL-6, TNF-alpha) and transient airway obstruction. This inflammatory response was a primary limiting factor that halted Phase 1/2 clinical advancement of mRNA and plasmid formulations (e.g. `NCT05668741` VX-522 program discontinuation).

Recent advances in minimal DNA technology (minicircles, Doggybone closed-ended linear DNA, and minidnacftr constructs; Porter et al., *Nucleic Acids Res.* 2026, DOI `10.1093/nar/gkag082`) eliminate bacterial origins of replication and antibiotic selection cassettes, enabling systematic CpG depletion while expressing engineered anticodon-edited suppressor tRNAs (ACE-tRNAs).

This specification directs the creation of a biophysical simulation tool in `cf-research` (`tools/discovery/minidna_evasion_model.py`) to model CpG dinucleotide density, TLR9 pattern-recognition activation flux, endosomal escape kinetics, and chromatinized episomal persistence in human bronchial epithelia.

## 3. Mathematical & Kinetic Model
1. **CpG Dinucleotide Frequency & Density**:
   $$\rho_{\text{CpG}} = \frac{N_{\text{CpG}}}{L_{\text{vector}}\ (\text{bp})}$$
   - Standard bacterial plasmid: $\rho_{\text{CpG}} \approx 0.05–0.08\text{ bp}^{-1}$ (~250–400 motifs in 5 kb).
   - Minimal DNA vector (minidnacftr): $\rho_{\text{CpG}} < 0.005\text{ bp}^{-1}$ (<5 motifs in 1.2 kb).
2. **TLR9 Activation Flux ($J_{\text{TLR9}}$)**:
   $$J_{\text{TLR9}} = k_{\text{TLR9}} \cdot N_{\text{CpG}} \cdot \frac{[V_{\text{endo}}]}{K_m + [V_{\text{endo}}]}$$
   Where $k_{\text{TLR9}}$ is the catalytic receptor turnover rate, and $[V_{\text{endo}}]$ is endosomal vector concentration.
3. **Endosomal Escape & Nuclear Entry**:
   $$\frac{d[V_{\text{cyt}}]}{dt} = k_{\text{esc}} [V_{\text{endo}}] - (k_{\text{deg,cyt}} + k_{\text{nuc}}) [V_{\text{cyt}}]$$
   $$\frac{d[V_{\text{nuc}}]}{dt} = k_{\text{nuc}} [V_{\text{cyt}}] - (k_{\text{silence}} + \mu_{\text{div}}) [V_{\text{nuc}}]$$
4. **Cytokine Reactogenicity Index ($R_{\text{cytokine}}$)**:
   $$R_{\text{cytokine}} = 1 - \exp\left(-\frac{J_{\text{TLR9}}}{J_{\text{safe}}}\right)$$
   Where $J_{\text{safe}}$ represents the clinical tolerability threshold below which macrophage activation remains undetectable.
