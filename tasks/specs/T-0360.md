## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/ptc/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling ribosome decoding center kinetics, premature termination codon (PTC) readthrough across CFTR Class I nonsense alleles (G542X, W1282X, R553X, R1162X), and amino acid misincorporation fidelity:
1. **Ribosome A-Site Kinetic Discrimination**: Models competitive binding between near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs and eukaryotic release factor complexes (eRF1-eRF3) under varying concentrations of readthrough agents (ELX-02, G418, ataluren/PTC124, and synthetic macrolides).
2. **Readthrough Insertion Profiling**: Visualizes the predicted amino acid substitution distribution (e.g. Trp vs Arg vs Cys vs Ser at UGA/UAA/UAG codons) and computes the resulting CFTR full-length channel folding stability and single-channel conductance.
3. **Translational Error Rate & Off-Target Window**: Explores global proteome misincorporation rates vs therapeutic CFTR rescue, highlighting the window between minimal curative chloride conductance (> 10-15% of wild-type) and cellular toxicity.
4. **Accessible, Dignity-Centered Design**: Built with responsive canvas visualization, ARIA live regions, keyboard navigation, and plain-language scientific explanations honoring CF patient community dignity.
