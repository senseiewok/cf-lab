# T-0264: CFTR RefSeq (NM_000492.4) Full Coding Sequence & Intronic Splice-Flank Verifier + Arbitrary-Allele gRNA/pegRNA/ASO/CpG Scanner

## Scientific & Curative Objective
Equip `cf-research/tools/discovery/` with a deterministic nucleotide-level sequence engine (`tools/discovery/cftr_sequence_scanner.py`) anchored in the canonical human *CFTR* RefSeq transcript (`NM_000492.4`, 4,443-bp coding sequence encoding 1,480 amino acids, UniProt `P13569`) and canonical intronic splice junctions (`3849+10kbC->T`, `2789+5G->A`, `1717-1G->A`).

While CF is monogenic (*CFTR* on chromosome 7q31.2), computational DNA/RNA sequence analysis is essential for mutation-specific and mutation-agnostic genetic therapies:
1. **Arbitrary-allele base & prime editing window verification**: Given any pathogenic *CFTR* variant (beyond hardcoded `W1282X`, `G542X`, `F508del`, `N1303K`), scan +/- 50 bp of surrounding genomic/cDNA sequence for SpCas9 (`NGG`), SpG (`NGN`), and SpRY PAM sites, compute exact protospacer positions for adenine/cytosine base editors (`ABE8e` positions 4–8) and prime editing (`pegRNA` PBS Tm and RTT length), and flag bystander adenines/cytosines that would cause missense collateral edits.
2. **Cryptic splice-site & ASO steric-blocking window scanner**: Score donor (`5' GT`) and acceptor (`3' AG`) splice consensus motifs and scan 18–25 nt antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) walk windows for Tm, GC%, self-dimer hairpin $\Delta G$, and poly-G quadruplex traps.
3. **TLR9 unmethylated CpG dinucleotide & mRNA codon optimization auditor**: Count immunostimulatory CpG motifs in non-viral minicircle/nanoplasmid DNA payloads and evaluate synonymous codon replacement options that preserve wild-type CFTR co-translational folding pauses at NBD1/R-domain boundaries.
