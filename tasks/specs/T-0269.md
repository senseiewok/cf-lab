## 1. Curative Mission & Scientific Scope

Extends `cftr_sequence_scanner.py` (`T-0264`) and `science_model_benchmark.py` (`T-0265`) to model the full panel of high-frequency Class V deep-intronic and consensus splice *CFTR* mutations (`3849+10kbC>T` / `c.3718-2477C>T`, `2789+5G>A` / `c.2657+5G>A`, `1811+1.6kbA>G` / `c.1680-886A>G`, and `3272-26A>G` / `c.3140-26A>G`), coupling **SpliceAI / Evo-2** donor-acceptor delta scores ($\Delta S_{\text{donor}}, \Delta S_{\text{acceptor}}$) with **2'-MOE / PMO** steric-blocking ASO dose-response curves and residual canonical transcript fraction ($f_{\text{WT}}$) needed to cross the 10–15% wild-type CFTR chloride transport threshold.
