## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops a biophysical simulation tool `tools/discovery/asl_osmotic_flux_model.py` and unit test suite `tools/discovery/test_asl_osmotic_flux_model.py` for mathematical modeling of ASL height and transepithelial water transport:
1. **ODE Transepithelial Water Flux System**: Formulates differential equations governing ASL height `$h(t)$`, sodium concentration `$[\text{Na}^+](t)$`, and chloride concentration `$[\text{Cl}^-](t)$` under resting, CF-dehydrated, and hypertonic stimulus conditions.
2. **Hydraulic Permeability & Osmotic Gradient**: Implements starling-type water flux $J_v = L_p (\Delta P - \sigma \Delta \Pi)$ where $L_p \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-7}\ \text{cm}/(\text{s}\cdot\text{cmH}_2\text{O})$ and $\sigma \approx 1.0$ for non-permeant macromolecules.
3. **Inhaled Hyperosmolar Challenge**: Simulates 3% vs 7% inhaled NaCl aerosol challenge, calculating time to peak ASL height and mucociliary clearance maintenance duration ($h \ge 7\ \mu\text{m}$).
4. **Anti-Hallucination & Discovery Registry**: Registers module in `discovery_council.py`, integrates with `tools/check_anti_hallucination.py` (AH-1..AH-5), and outputs deterministic JSON results.
