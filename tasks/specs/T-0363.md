## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/club_cell_secretoglobin_model.py` to model bronchiolar club cell (SCGB1A1+/CCSP+) secretion kinetics, uteroglobin anti-inflammatory dynamics, and surfactant protein homeostasis in Cystic Fibrosis airways:
1. **Club Cell Secretory Profiling**: Models baseline and cytokine-perturbed secretion of CC16 (club cell 16 kDa secretory protein), surfactant proteins SP-A and SP-D, and CYP450 detoxification enzymes across healthy vs CF distal airway epithelia.
2. **Anti-Inflammatory & Anti-Protease Stoichiometry**: Quantifies CC16 inhibition of soluble phospholipase A2 (sPLA2) and its downstream modulation of neutrophil chemotaxis and elastase-mediated mucosal damage.
3. **Regenerative Plasticity & Club-to-Ciliated Differentiation**: Models the regenerative lineage differentiation of club cells following epithelial denudation and calculates the kinetic barrier restoration under CFTR-corrected vs CFTR-deficient states.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_club_cell_secretoglobin.py` and JSON schema validation for export.
