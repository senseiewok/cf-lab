## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a mechanistic biochemical model in `tools/discovery/cftr_snitrosylation_pdi_model.py` evaluating the redox-sensitive S-nitrosylation of conserved CFTR transmembrane cysteine residues and their enzymatic regulation by Protein Disulfide Isomerase (PDI):
1. **Cryptic Cysteine Nitrosylation Dynamics**: Quantifies S-nitrosylation equilibrium at Cys524 (TMD1-NBD1 coupling loop) and Cys1355 (NBD2) under fluctuating airway GSNO / NO ratios and oxidative stress.
2. **PDI-Mediated Catalytic Reduction**: Models PDI / PDIA1 oxidoreductase turnover kinetics, coupling PDI thiol-disulfide exchange with CFTR conformational stability.
3. **Misfolding & Corrector Rescue Synergy**: Simulates how selective denitrosylation synergizes with Class I/II correctors (lumacaftor, tezacaftor) to prevent premature endoplasmic reticulum-associated degradation (ERAD) of $\Delta$F508-CFTR.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive test suite in `tools/discovery/test_cftr_snitrosylation_pdi_model.py`.
