## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tools/discovery/lnp_shear_stability_model.py` to model the aerosolization and nebulization shear stability of synthetic CFTR mRNA lipid nanoparticles (LNPs):
1. **Nebulization Shear & Surface Tension Stress**: Quantifies hydrodynamic shear rates (10^4 to 10^6 s^-1) generated across vibrating mesh (e.g. eFlow/Aerogen) vs jet nebulizers and their destabilizing force on the lipid bilayer shell.
2. **PEG-Lipid Desorption & Core Unpackaging Kinetics**: Models the rate of PEG-lipid dissociation during acoustic droplet formation, predicting post-nebulization vesicle aggregation, payload mRNA leak, and loss of mucosal steric stealth.
3. **Formulation Optimization Space**: Simulates varied molar fractions of ionizable lipid, helper phospholipid (DOPE/DSPC), cholesterol, and anchor-length PEG-lipids (e.g. DMG-PEG2000 vs DSPE-PEG2000) to maximize intact delivery flux into deep airway epithelia.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_lnp_shear_stability.py` with reproducible biophysical constants and boundary conditions.
