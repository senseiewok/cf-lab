## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/suppressor_trna_orthogonality.py` modeling nonsense mutation suppression:
1. **Ribosomal A-Site Competition**: Models kinetic competition between engineered suppressor aminoacyl-tRNA and release factor eRF1-eRF3 complex at premature `UGA` stop codons (W1282X, G542X).
2. **Transcriptome Orthogonality Index**: Formulates global selectivity ratio ($OI \ge 10^2$) safeguarding natural termination codons.
3. **Coupled Mature Channel Expression**: Integrates transcript escape from nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) with translational elongation velocity.
