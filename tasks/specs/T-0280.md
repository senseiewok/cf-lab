## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#gating-potentiator-explorer` on `/cf/cftr/` to simulate allosteric cooperativity and energetic coupling between dual CFTR potentiators (Site 1 / TMD8/11 ivacaftor pocket vs. Site 2 / NBD-TMD interface booster) across gating (Class III: G551D, G1244E) and gating-impaired (Class IV: R117H; Class II rescued F508del) alleles. Couples free-energy linkage ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{open}} = -k_B T \ln(K_{\text{eq}} / K_{\text{eq},0})$) with thermodynamic cycle shifts, open probability ($P_o$) Hill cooperative curves, and burst duration kinetics. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity and accessible SVG dose-response curves.
