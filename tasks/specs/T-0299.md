## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Discovery Scope

Mounts `#u1-decoy-splice-explorer` on `/cf/ptc/` to showcase competitive thermodynamics and kinetic exclusion of deep-intronic pseudoexons in CFTR pre-mRNA (specifically 3849+10kb C->T and 1811+1.6kb A->G) grounded in `tools/discovery/u1_snrnp_decoy_kinetics.py` (T-0292):
1. **Competitive Nearest-Neighbor Duplex Thermodynamics**: Simulates the free-energy displacement ($\Delta\Delta G^\circ$) of U1 snRNP from the cryptic 5' donor site by 2'-MOE and PMO splice-switching oligonucleotides (SSOs, e.g. SPL84).
2. **Kinetic Partitioning & Full-Length mRNA Restoration**: Models fractional site occupancy $\theta_{\text{SSO}}$ vs $\theta_{\text{U1}}$ and computes dose-dependent recovery of productive CFTR mRNA ($EC_{50} \sim 20\text{--}80\text{ nM}$) up to >85% of normal physiological levels.
3. **Transepithelial Conductance Restoration**: Connects restored mRNA to functional apical Band C CFTR channel density, estimating short-circuit current ($\Delta I_{sc}$) and equivalent conductance ($G_{\text{Cl}}$).
4. **Mexican Spanish Parity (`es-MX`)**: Full bilingual UI cards and labels in `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js` with dignity boundary disclaimers.
5. **Strict Zero-Build & CSP Hygiene**: Built with native ES modules, accessible responsive SVG graphics, and zero unsafe DOM injection (no `innerHTML`/`outerHTML`).
