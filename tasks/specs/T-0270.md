## 1. Curative Mission & Scientific Scope

Models allosteric energetic cooperativity ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{coupling}}$) and conformational gating transitions of human CFTR under single and dual potentiator regimens across severe gating alleles (**G551D**, **G1244E**, **S549N**). Couples a **Two-State Allosteric Monod-Wyman-Changeux (MWC) Gating Equilibrium** with **state-dependent potentiator binding** at the lipid-facing TM8/TM4/TM5 hinge cavity (PDB: 6O2P, 7SVD, 8EIQ), **dual-potentiator allosteric cooperativity**, and **thermodynamic double-mutant cycle analysis** to quantify energetic coupling between binding pocket residues and catalytic gating switches.
