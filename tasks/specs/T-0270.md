## 1. Curative Mission & User Value

Translates the newly verified Class V deep-intronic cryptic exon benchmark (`T-0269`, `intronic_splice_foundation.py`) into an interactive bilingual (EN / `es-MX`) research card and dose-response simulator mounted on `/cf/ptc/`. Enables families, clinicians, and researchers to explore how **SpliceAI** donor/acceptor delta scores ($\Delta S_{\text{donor}}, \Delta S_{\text{acceptor}}$) and **Evo-2** zero-shot genomic likelihoods predict pseudoexon inclusion across `3849+10kbC>T`, `2789+5G>A`, `1811+1.6kbA>G`, and `3272-26A>G`. Simulates 2'-MOE and PMO steric-blocking ASO concentration curves that restore canonical transcript fraction $f_{\text{WT}}$ across the 10–15% wild-type curative threshold.
