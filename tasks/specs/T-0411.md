## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive browser simulation widget on `senseiewok.ai/cf/ptc/` translating the biophysical discoveries of `T-0394` (engineered suppressor tRNA readthrough kinetics and transcriptome orthogonality) to public understanding:
1. **Interactive Suppressor tRNA Dosing Controls**: Sliders modulating tRNA expression level ($0.1\text{--}10.0\ \mu\text{M}$), anticodon mutation pairing (UGA Opal suppression for W1282X, UAA Ochre for G542X), and aminoacylation efficiency ($\eta_{\text{chg}}$).
2. **Ribosomal A-Site Competition Live Readout**: Live competition model between engineered suppressor tRNA and eRF1/eRF3 termination complex, displaying readthrough efficiency ($F_{\text{read}}$) alongside natural stop codon readthrough penalty ($< 1.0\%$).
3. **Mature Band C Channel Recovery**: Quantitative projection of restored 170 kDa mature glycosylated CFTR protein and apical chloride conductance ($\% \text{WT } I_{\text{sc}}$), demonstrating restoration above the $10\%\text{ WT}$ clinical threshold.
4. **Bilingual Parity & Dignity**: Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) localization with exact medical boundaries, zero clinical efficacy claims, and clear distinctions between in vitro models and clinical trials.
