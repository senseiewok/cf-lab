## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/asl_ph_regulation_model.py` to simulate steady-state ASL proton concentrations and pH dynamics under varying CFTR and proton-translocating ATPase activities:
1. **Transepithelial Proton-Bicarbonate Flux Equations**: Models steady-state pH via the Henderson-Hasselbalch formulation coupled to apical ion transporters:
   $$J_{\text{net}}(\text{H}^+) = J_{\text{ATP12A}}(\text{H}^+) + J_{\text{V-ATPase}}(\text{H}^+) - J_{\text{CFTR}}(\text{HCO}_3^-) - J_{\text{bicarb}}(\text{SLC26A4/A9})$$
2. **Buffer Capacity & Carbonic Anhydrase Kinetics**: Solves chemical equilibria for the $\text{CO}_2 / \text{HCO}_3^-$ open buffer system ($5\%\,\text{CO}_2$, $P_{\text{CO}_2} = 38\,\text{mmHg}$) and non-bicarbonate protein/mucus buffering capacity ($\beta_{\text{nb}} \approx 5-15\,\text{mM}/\text{pH unit}$).
3. **Small-Molecule ATP12A Inhibitor Rescue Profile**: Computes the half-maximal inhibitory concentration ($\text{IC}_{50}$) and therapeutic window required for topical ATP12A blockers (ouabain, SCH-28080 analogs) to restore ASL pH from $6.75$ to $>7.25$, restoring bacterial killing activity against *Staphylococcus aureus* and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_asl_ph_regulation_model.py` and registers check rows in `cf-research` test runners.
