## 1. Curative Mission & Discovery Scope

Delivers `tools/discovery/nbd1_tmd_pharmacophore.py` and unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_nbd1_tmd_pharmacophore.py` to quantitatively model allosteric rescue of the human CFTR $\Delta\text{F508}$ (c.1521_1523delCTT) misfolding and trafficking defect at the intracellular loop 4 (ICL4) / NBD1 coupling interface. Evaluates free energy thermodynamic stabilization ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{fold}}$), corrector synergy matrices (lumacaftor VX-809, tezacaftor VX-661, elexacaftor VX-445, and next-generation candidate combos), and computes predicted cell-surface Band C maturation fractions in primary human bronchial epithelial cells. Registers module in `tools/discovery/discovery_council.py`.
