## 1. Operational Security & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Implements `tasks/check_catalog_schema_lock.py` to validate evidence source integrity and catalog schema consistency across all sibling repositories:
1. Validates `cf-research/sources/catalog.yaml` and `cf-skills/.claude/skills/cf-evidence-loop/catalog.yaml` against the canonical evidence catalog JSON schema.
2. Ensures license declarations, terms-of-use flags, and programmatic access permissions (`manual`, `request`, `forbidden`, `open`) conform strictly to `security-browsing` and `security-baseline`.
3. Verifies cryptographic pin consistency (`sources/catalog.lock.json`) to prevent unreviewed source modifications or unauthorized scraper access.
4. Generates deterministic diagnostic report in `.loop-logs/catalog-schema-audit.json`.

## 2. Primary Source Grounding

1. **cf-research/sources/catalog.yaml**: Canonical single source catalog of external medical and registry evidence sources.
2. **.claude/skills/security-browsing/SKILL.md**: Web browsing, scraping, rate limits, and terms-of-use compliance rules.
3. **.claude/skills/security-baseline/SKILL.md**: Medical research data integrity and permission enforcement.
