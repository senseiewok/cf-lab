## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers interactive 3-engine cross-scale discovery synthesis and printable care-team visit-prep sheet support on `/cf/trials/`:
1. **Engine A (Genotype x Recruiting Trial Gap Matrix)**: Connects 209 indexed CFTR2 variants, Cryo-EM atomic structures (6MSM, 5UAK, 8EIQ, 8EJ1), and 54,043 patients in the ECFSPR longitudinal cohort to interventional trials, highlighting that exactly 5 of 57 recruiting trials (8.77%) evaluate mutation-agnostic genetic replacement (such as NCT05668741 [VX-522] and NCT05248230 [4D-710]).
2. **Engine B (Modality x Endpoint Compartmentalization)**: Synthesizes 102 non-zero primary outcome pairs from 1,072 interventional studies in snapshot `b0b50e6cf45f`, explaining why FEV1 and sweat chloride co-occur as co-primary outcomes in only 4 trials (1.53% of FEV1 trials).
3. **Engine C (Curative Delivery Bottleneck)**: Connects 52 verified discovery models in `tools/discovery/` to interventional trials, showing that in vitro molecular current recovery is rate-limited in clinic by the 317.0x diffusivity collapse of unshielded vectors in CF sputum relative to muco-inert PEGylated SORT-LNPs ($D_{\text{eff}}/D_0 = 0.268$ vs $0.00085$).
4. **Printable CF Clinic Visit-Prep Sheet**: Adds dedicated "Print Visit-Prep Sheet" button (`#btn-print-sheet`) wired to `window.print()` and responsive `@media print` styling in `src/styles-trials.css` hiding website chrome for clean 1-page clinic handouts.
5. **Bilingual Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation across `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js` with exact clinical boundaries.
