## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops `tools/discovery/ciliary_wave_clearance_model.py` to quantify mucociliary transport velocity ($v_{\text{MCT}}$, $\text{mm/min}$) as a function of coordinated ciliary metachrony, PCL height, and mucus rheology:
1. **Stokeslet Hydrodynamic Flow Field**: Solves the near-wall Stokes equation for an array of beating ciliary axonemes with effective power-stroke and recovery-stroke asymmetry, deriving the net volume transport rate $Q = \int_0^h u(z)\,dz$.
2. **Coupled PCL-Mucus Two-Phase Hydrodynamics**: Models momentum transfer across the PCL-mucus interface with discontinuous viscosity ($\eta_{\text{PCL}} \approx 1\,\text{mPa}\cdot\text{s}$ vs. $\eta_{\text{mucus}} \approx 10^3-10^5\,\text{mPa}\cdot\text{s}$).
3. **Cystic Fibrosis Stasis Threshold**: Calculates the critical threshold of PCL dehydration ($h_{\text{PCL}} < 4\,\mu\text{m}$) and mucus solids percentage ($>8\,\text{wt}\%$) at which metachronal wave propagation arrests and clearance drops to zero.
4. **Deterministic Testing**: Includes comprehensive unit tests in `tools/discovery/test_ciliary_wave_clearance_model.py` and registers check rows in `cf-research` test runners.
