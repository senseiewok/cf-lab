## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Develops an interactive biophysical Calu-3 ALI culture and drug permeation simulator on `src/cf/delivery/index.html` and parallel Mexican Spanish route `src/es/cf/delivery/index.html`:
1. **ALI Differentiation & TEER Dynamics**: Models transepithelial electrical resistance ($\text{TEER} \approx 400-1200\ \Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$) maturation over 21-day air-liquid interface culture, showing tight junction strand formation (Claudin-1, Claudin-4, ZO-1).
2. **Transepithelial Drug Permeability**: Simulates apical-to-basolateral drug flux ($J$) and calculates apparent permeability coefficients ($P_{\text{app}} = (\Delta Q / \Delta t) / (A \cdot C_0)$) across small molecules, peptide modulators, and macromolecular nanocarriers.
3. **Interactive Experimental Controls**: Sliders for air-exposure time (days 0-28), tight junction opening factor (hyperosmolar mannitol / chitosan / zonula occludens toxin), and donor drug initial concentration ($C_0$).
4. **Bilingual Dignity & Parity**: Full Mexican Spanish localization with reciprocal `hreflang` tags, `<html lang="es-MX">`, and the verified non-advice research boundary disclaimer.
5. **Deterministic Verification**: Unit tests in `tests/test_ali_teer_simulator.py` and anti-hallucination verification (AH-1..AH-5).
