# T-0219: Base Editing (ABE8e / CBE) Bystander Deamination Window & PAM Accessibility Model for CFTR Alleles

## 1. Overview & Curative Mission Focus (Track A)
Nonsense mutations (`W1282X`, `R553X`, `G542X`) and canonical splice junction mutations (e.g., `2789+5G->A`) account for a critical subset of Cystic Fibrosis patients ineligible for or sub-optimally responsive to small-molecule CFTR modulators. Adenine Base Editors (e.g. `ABE8e`) and Cytosine Base Editors (`CBE`) enable transition mutations (A·T to G·C or C·G to T·A) without double-strand DNA breaks. However, therapeutic safety and efficacy hinge upon:
1. SpCas9 / SpRY / PAM variant accessibility ($NGG$, $NRN$, $NYN$).
2. The deamination activity window within the protospacer (positions 4–8 for canonical editors; positions 3–9 for ABE8e).
3. Off-target bystander edit kinetics (introducing missense perturbations into adjacent codons of CFTR).

This specification directs the implementation of a deterministic biophysical model in `cf-research` to evaluate base editing guide RNAs (gRNAs) across priority refractory CFTR mutations.

## 2. Primary Evidence Grounding
- **Biophysical Evolution**: Richter et al., "Phage-assisted evolution of an adenine base editor with greatly increased on-target activity", *Nature Biotechnology*, 2020. DOI: `10.1038/s41587-020-0453-z`.
- **Structural CFTR Coordinates**: Human CFTR dephosphorylated, ATP-free conformation resolved by Cryo-EM. PDB: `6MSM`.
- **Clinical Trial Pipeline**: Clinical evaluation of investigational base and prime editing therapeutic agents in genetic respiratory disease. ClinicalTrials.gov ID: `NCT06237335`.
- **CFTR Alleles**:
  - `W1282X`: c.3846G>A (TGA stop codon in Exon 23); target A on non-coding strand: ABE restores TGG (Trp).
  - `R553X`: c.1657C>T (TGA stop codon in Exon 12 / NBD1); target A on non-coding strand: ABE restores CGA (Arg).
  - `G542X`: c.1624G>T (TGA stop codon in Exon 12 / NBD1); transversion allele requiring dual deaminase or prime editing.
  - `2789+5G->A`: c.2657+5G>A (intronic splice site donor); target A on non-coding strand for splice restoration.

## 3. Mathematical & Biophysical Model
Let protospacer position index be $p \in [1, 20]$ (where $1$ is 5' distal and $20$ is adjacent to PAM at $21-23$ $NGG$).
The deamination probability profile for editor $E \in \{\text{ABE7.10}, \text{ABE8e}, \text{AncBE4max}\}$ is modeled as an asymmetric Gaussian editing window:
$$W_E(p) = \exp\left(-\frac{(p - p_{0,E})^2}{2\sigma_E^2}\right)$$
where for `ABE8e`:
$$p_{0,ABE8e} \approx 5.5, \quad \sigma_{ABE8e} \approx 1.8 \quad (\text{effective window: positions } 3 \text{ to } 9)$$

For a target adenine $A_t$ at position $p_t$ and bystander adenines $A_b \in \mathcal{B}$ at positions $p_b$:
$$\text{Efficiency}(A_t) = W_E(p_t) \cdot k_{\text{PAM}}(\text{PAM}) \cdot \Lambda_{\text{chromatin}}$$
The bystander purity ratio $\mathcal{P}$ is defined as:
$$\mathcal{P} = \frac{\text{Efficiency}(A_t)}{\text{Efficiency}(A_t) + \sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}} W_E(p_b)}$$
Predicted amino acid outcomes are evaluated against Cryo-EM structural coordinates (`PDB 6MSM`) to classify bystander missense mutations as benign, tolerated, or structurally destabilizing ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{fold}} > 2.0\text{ kcal/mol}$).
