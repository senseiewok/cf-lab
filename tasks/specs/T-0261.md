## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#mpp-scaling-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate aerosolized nanoparticle transport through diseased CF sputum hydrogels. Models Ogston steric hydrogel mesh filtering across physiological pore sizes ($\xi = 60\text{--}300\text{ nm}$), dense PEG brush corona shielding ($\Gamma / \Gamma^* \ge 2.0$) minimizing mucin adhesive trapping, and rhDNase I (dornase alfa) extracellular DNA digestion expanding effective mesh diameter. Provides real-time effective diffusivity ratio ($D_{\text{eff}} / D_0$), cumulative 60-minute epithelial delivery fraction, and 10% curative threshold benchmark. Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
