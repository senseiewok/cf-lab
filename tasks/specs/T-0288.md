## 1. Curative Mission & Public Educational Scope

Mounts `#lnp-escape-dynamics-explorer` on `/cf/delivery/` to simulate the multi-barrier bottleneck of lipid nanoparticle (LNP) endosomal escape and cytosolic mRNA uncoating in human airway basal stem cells. Models endosomal acidification kinetics ($pH\ 7.4 \rightarrow 5.0$), ionizable lipid headgroup protonation (apparent $pK_a \sim 6.4$), cone-to-inverted hexagonal ($H_{II}$) phase transition, membrane fusion and pore rupture kinetics, and cytosolic dissociation from packaging lipids. Accessible SVG phase diagram and full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation parity.
