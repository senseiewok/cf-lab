## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers a quantitative biophysical model and unit test suite in `tools/discovery/split_intein_cftr_reconstitution.py` modeling dual-AAV split-intein protein trans-splicing:
1. **Co-Transduction Poisson Kinetics**: Models joint probability of N- and C-terminal vector delivery across airway epithelial cells under varying MOI.
2. **Second-Order Intein Pairing & Trans-Splicing**: Implements association and autocatalytic splicing kinetics yielding mature 170 kDa functional CFTR.
3. **Thermodynamic Stability & Redosing Neutralization**: Analyzes intein folding free energy and evaluates delivery attenuation under accumulating neutralizing antibody titers.
