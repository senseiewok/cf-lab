## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/ptc/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling splice-switching antisense oligonucleotide (AON/ASO) binding thermodynamics, cryptic pseudoexon steric occlusion, and reading-frame rescue for deep-intronic CFTR mutations (e.g. c.3718-2477C>T / 3849+10kbC>T):
1. **Splice-Site Thermodynamic Scoring**: Computes nearest-neighbor RNA duplex delta G, GC-content, melting temperature (Tm), and target accessibility for candidate 2'-O-methyl / phosphorothioate (2'-OMe-PS) and phosphorodiamidate morpholino oligomer (PMO) chemistries.
2. **Steric Occlusion of Cryptic Splicing Enhancers**: Models competitive displacement of U1 snRNP and serine/arginine-rich (SR) splicing factor proteins (SRSF1/SF2, SRSF2/SC35) from intronic splicing enhancer (ISE) motifs.
3. **Percentage In-Frame Transcript & CFTR Function Restoration**: Simulates the dose-response relationship between intranuclear AON concentration, cryptic exon skipping efficiency (percent spliced in, PSI), and resulting apical chloride conductance (% wild-type CFTR).
4. **Accessible, Zero-Build Architecture**: Implements native ES modules with accessible interactive thermodynamic sliders, SVG duplex schematic, keyboard controls, ARIA landmarks, and strict dignity language boundaries.
