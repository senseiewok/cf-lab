## 1. Curative Mission & Biophysical Scope

Builds an interactive pharmacodynamic simulation on `senseiewok.ai/cf/ptc/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` translation parity at `/es/cf/ptc/`) exploring premature termination codon (PTC) readthrough kinetics and toxicity windows:
1. **Dose-Response Kinetics**: Models translational readthrough efficiency across aminoglycosides (G418, gentamicin), designer eukaryotic readthrough inducers (ELX-02 / NB124), and oxadiazoles (ataluren) across CFTR W1282X, G542X, R553X, and R1162X alleles.
2. **Therapeutic vs Toxic Window**: Interactively illustrates the concentration threshold between desired PTC readthrough (restoring full-length CFTR protein) and severe off-target readthrough of normal termination codons, mitochondrial ribosome mistranslation, and ototoxicity/nephrotoxicity.
3. **Synergy with NMD Inhibitors & Suppressor tRNAs**: Demonstrates how combining low-dose readthrough inducers with SMG1 inhibitors or suppressor ACE-tRNAs expands the therapeutic safety margin by >10-fold.
4. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility**: Accessible slider controls, real-time Canvas concentration-response curves, live ARIA metric badges, and complete bilingual copy.
