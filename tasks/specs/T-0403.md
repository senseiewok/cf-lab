## 1. Curative Mission & Architecture Stewardship Scope

Delivers an interactive, zero-build client-side visualization on `/cf/ptc/` (with complete Mexican Spanish `es-MX` parity) modeling ribosomal decoding competition and small-molecule release factor depletion for Class I nonsense alleles:
1. **Ribosomal A-Site Decoding Competition**: Models kinetic competition between active ternary termination complexes (eRF1-eRF3-GTP) and near-cognate aminoacyl-tRNAs (Trp, Cys, Arg).
2. **Release Factor Depletion Dynamics**: Simulates small-molecule release factor depleters (SRI-37240 / SRI-41315) across Hill cooperativity ($h = 1.8$, $\text{IC}_{50} = 0.45\ \mu\text{M}$) and super-linear reduction in ternary termination complexes ($[\text{eRF}]_{\text{complex}} \sim [\text{eRF}]^{1.6}$).
3. **Premature Stop Codon Permissiveness**: Evaluates codon termination hierarchy ($W1282X$ UGA+G context, $G542X$ UGA+A context, $R553X$, $R1162X$, $Y122X$ UAA+U context), pioneer round NMD escape, and mature Band C CFTR channel expression rescue crossing the curative threshold ($\ge 10.0\%$ WT current).
4. **Complete Localization**: Full Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`) translation dictionary in `data/i18n/es-MX.json` and `src/scripts/i18n-resources.js` with zero prohibited words.
