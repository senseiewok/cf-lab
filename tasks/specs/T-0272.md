## 1. Curative Mission & Scientific Scope

Models translation dynamics of full-length synthetic human CFTR mRNA (4,443 nt / 1,480 codons) delivered via aerosolized lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) to airway epithelia. Couples **Totally Asymmetric Simple Exclusion Process (TASEP)** steric ribosome exclusion (~10 codons footprint) with **poly(A) tail deadenylation kinetics** (PAN2-PAN3 protected vs CCR4-NOT unprotected phase), **PABP circularization loop synergy**, and **co-translational domain folding coordination** across MSD1, NBD1, R, MSD2, and NBD2 domains to maximize functional CFTR channel yield while preventing ribosome collision-induced No-Go Decay (NGD).
